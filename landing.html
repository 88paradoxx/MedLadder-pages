How it works

Answer, read why, come back to what you missed

Testing yourself and spacing the repeats out are two of the best-supported ways to remember what you study. MedLadder is built around both.

1

Pick a topic

Every subject is split into short topics, from Gametogenesis to Glaucoma, each a focused set of exam-style questions.

2

Read the explanation

Each answer comes with the reasoning and the textbook it draws on, so a wrong answer still teaches you something.

3

Revisit what you missed

Your accuracy is tracked topic by topic, so you can see what needs another round and retry the questions you got wrong.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How many free modules do I get?

You get the first 8 topic modules completely free in every single one of the 19 subjects — that's 152 modules unlocked immediately with full question sets and explanations without entering any payment info.

Which exams are covered in MedLadder?

MedLadder is built for NEET PG, INI-CET, FMGE and NEET SS. The 19-subject bank follows the MBBS syllabus these exams draw on, and the past papers cover NEET PG, INI-CET, FMGE, AIIMS PG and NEET SS.

What is the difference between Practice and Exam modes?

Practice Mode reveals the correct answer immediately after you click an option along with clinical pearls and explanations. Exam Mode simulates a real test with a customizable countdown timer, answer review, and standard negative marking.

Can I practice on my mobile or tablet?

Yes. MedLadder is a fully responsive Progressive Web App. You can log into your account from your iPhone, Android phone, iPad, tablet, or desktop and your answered questions and scores will sync automatically.
