Sample MCQ #1

A 50-year-old overweight woman constable presents with amenorrhoea of 6 months' duration. There is no family history of osteoporosis or fracture. She does not smoke and is a teetotaler. Blood tests: FSH 35 IU/l (postmenopausal level > 30), LH 57 IU/l (Postmenopausal level > 30). Bone density measurements with a DEXA scan are within normal limits. What is the chief reason that this woman has not developed osteoporosis?

AContinued physical activity
BAbsence of positive family history
CNon-smoker
DOverweight condition
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Overweight condition

All the options contribute to a decreased incidence of osteoporosis. However, the fact that she is overweight suggests that her estrone levels are high due to higher expression of aromatase enzyme present in adipose tissue. Estrone is the main sex hormone produced after the menopause. Ovarian thecal androgens are converted to estrone in adipose tissue by the enzyme aromatase. High levels of estrone in this woman may thus be the main factor preventing the occurrence of bone resorption and osteoporosis. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine 21 st edition Page 3195

Sample MCQ #7

A 58-year-old woman is referred by the Ortho department after attending with a vertebral fracture, the second in the last 3 years. She underwent a total hysterectomy with oophorectomy at the age of 38 for carcinoma. Bone densitometry confirms osteoporosis. The calcium and parathyroid hormone assays are normal. Which of the following is the most likely concerning her underlying pathophysiology?

AShe has suffered predominantly cortical bone loss
BShe has probably suffered an equal mix of cortical and trabecular bone loss
CShe has probably suffered predominantly trabecular bone loss
DUnderlying hyperparathyroidism will have contributed
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. She has probably suffered predominantly trabecular bone loss

Hypo-estrogenization, as in this case, is usually characterized by predominantly trabecular bone loss. Involutional bone loss of old age is characterized by a mixed picture of trabecular and cortical bone loss. In the presence of normal calcium and parathyroid hormone levels, parathyroid disease is ruled out. Hormone replacement therapy was formerly the mainstay of treatment, but this has fallen out of favor due to increased thromboembolic disease and the risk of breast carcinoma. The current line of therapy for osteoporosis include either anti-resorption agents like bisphosphonates and denosumab or anabolic agents like Teriparatide. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine 21 st edition Page 3192-93

Sample MCQ #8

Which one of the following statements is true for the actions of parathyroid hormone (PTH)?

AActs directly on renal, bone, and intestinal cells
BActs directly on renal cells and indirectly on bone and intestinal cells
CActs directly on renal and bone cells and indirectly on intestinal cells
DHas no direct actions on renal, bone, and intestinal cells
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Acts directly on renal and bone cells and indirectly on intestinal cells

PTH shares a receptor with PTH-related peptide (PTHrP), and this receptor is a member of the G protein&ndash;coupled receptor (GPCR) family. PTH/PTHrP receptors are expressed by kidney and bone cells, and PTH acts directly on renal and bone cells. PTH acts indirectly on intestinal cells to enhance calcium absorption as follows: PTH stimulates renal 1&alpha;-hydroxylase activity to promote synthesis of the active form of vitamin D (1,25-dihydroxyvitamin D), which then acts on the intestine to enhance calcium absorption. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine 21st edition, page number 3171
