Sample MCQ #1

A 37-year-old female presents with headaches. Examination reveals papilloedema. Which of the following would make the diagnosis of benign intracranial hypertension (BIH) unlikely?

AAbsence of retinal venous pulsations
BBilateral up going plantar responses
CNormal ventricles on CT or MRI scan
DReduced visual acuity
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Bilateral up going plantar responses

BIH is typically associated with papilloedema, reduced venous pulsation and normal appearances of the magnetic resonance imaging (MRI). A VIth nerve palsy is a recognised association. Reflexes are preserved and plantars are flexor. Extensor plantars suggest an alternative diagnosis. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21 st Ed, Ch: 16Pg: 116

Sample MCQ #2

A 30 year old male comes to you with history of recurrent retro orbital pains that occurs once or twice in a day, each lasting for around an hour, associated with ipsilateral lacrimation. He has had similar episodes before which occurred for a period of 1week, after which he remains asymptomatic for around 6-8 months. Which of the following statements is false regarding the preventive therapy of the above condition?

ASince the episodes lasts only for around 3 weeks, steroids are a good option for preventive therapy
BVerapamil is the first line treatment for episodes that lasts longer
CLithium is a drug that is of proven benefit in preventing the episodes of this head ache
DCGRP monoclonal antibody is useful in chronic cluster head ache
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. CGRP monoclonal antibody is useful in chronic cluster head ache

The patient in the question suffers from cluster head ache. The bouts or episodes occur for a very short period of 1 weeks. The preventive therapy of cluster head ache depends up on the duration of bouts. For shorter bouts the drugs that are approved are: Prednisolone CGRP monoclonal antibody Galcanezumab Great Occipital nerve injection with lignocaine or steroids Verapamil For bouts that are longer, the drugs that are approved are Verapamil 160-960mg/day Lithium 400-800mg/day Non invasive vagal stimulation Deep brain stimulation Melatonin, Topiramate and Gabapentin are of unproven benefits So among the options, the CGRP monoclonal antibody is useful not for chronic cluster head ache or longer bouts, but for shorter bouts of head ache. Reference : Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21 st ed, Ch: 430, Pg: 3367

Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following statements regarding CADASIL (cerebral autosomal dominant arteriopathy with subcortical infarcts and leukoencephalopathy) is correct?

AComputerised tomography (CT) of the brain is diagnostic
BThalami are usually spared
CIt is associated with an abnormality of the X chromosome
DIt may present with migraine
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. It may present with migraine

CADASIL is a diffuse disease of small arteries, predominantly in the brain. It starts during mid adulthood and is characterised by: Recurrent ischaemic events (transient or permanent) Attacks of migraine with aura Severe mood disorders Sub-cortical dementia and A wide spread leukoencephalopathy on magnetic resonance imaging (MRI). CT brain is not diagnostic. Characteristic MRI changes include T2 weighted hyperintensity of the periventricular white matter. A family history is almost always present, as it is an autosomal dominant condition, located to chromosome 19. Infarcts are subcortical, and the thalami and basal ganglia are usually involved. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21 st ed, Ch: 427, Pg: 3343

Sample MCQ #5

A 78-year-old lady presents with a 5 day history of severe left temporal headache radiating from her eye to the scalp. She had also experienced jaw discomfort during eating. Which of the following drugs should be given while awaiting results of diagnostic tests?

ACarbamazepine
BPrednisolone
CAzathioprine
DInfliximab
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Prednisolone

The history suggests temporal arteritis. In view of the vision threatening nature of disease, the patient should be commenced on steroids, pending the confirmatory tests. Typically 60 mg of prednisolone per day is recommended. Reference: Harrison&rsquo;s Principles of Internal Medicine 21 st edition Ch:363 Pg:2811-2812

Sample MCQ #6

An elderly woman on treatment for migraine presents with new onset chest pain, without any previous history of angina/MI. The drug with which of the following mechanisms of action might have caused this presentation?

A5 HT Agonist
B5 HT antagonist
CCalcium channel blockade
DNitric oxide mediated vasodilation
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. 5 HT Agonist

Triptans are a group of anti migraine drugs that cause vasoconstriction. So they have the propensity to precipitate angina.

Sample MCQ #7

A 31-year-old woman has known migraine. She gets periodic episodes of headaches with associated visual symptoms. Which one of the Following drugs should be used first in a migraine attack?

AIbuprofen
BMethysergide
CSubcutaneous sumatriptan
DOral sumatriptan
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Ibuprofen

In acute migraine attack, the first line treatments are simple analgesics such as aspirin, ibuprofen or paracetamol. Second line treatment in acute migraine are the triptans (e.g. sumatriptan) which work by selectively stimulating 5-hydroxytriptamine 1 (5HT1) receptors. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine 21 st edition Ch: 430, Pg: 3361
