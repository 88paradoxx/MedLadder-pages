Sample MCQ #1

When exposed to positive events, like going on a holiday or receiving a compliment, transient upliftment of mood is seen in depressed patients. This is termed as ____________.

AInappropriate affect
BReactivity of mood
CMood Lability
DRestricted affect
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Reactivity of mood

Reactivity of mood is the term used to describe the fluctuations in mood that occur in parallel with changes to one's environment Mood will improve on going on holiday or when the stressful situation alters An example, when difficulties with a workmate resolve when that person resigns, but recur when the subject is re-exposed to the stressful situation Reference : Fish's Clinical Psychopathology, third edition, Chapter 5 Disorders of emotion, Page no. 74

Sample MCQ #2

In a patient with Melancholic depression, the mood is characteristically described as _______________.

ADysthymic
BDepressed
CDespondent
DReactive
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Despondent

A distinct quality of depressed mood characterized by profound despondency, despair, and/or moroseness or by ; so-called empty mood is a characteristic feature of Melancholic depression Reference: Kaplan and Sadock's Synopsis of Psychiatry, Eleventh edition, Chapter 8, Mood Disorders, Page 362

Sample MCQ #3

How long after a stroke is a patient at a higher risk for developing a depressive disorder?

A2 weeks
B2 months
C6 months
D2 years
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. 2 years

Studies of the course and prognosis of post stroke depression indicate that the high-risk period can last up to 2 years. The presence of depression is associated with an eightfold increase in mortality risk. Reference : Kaplan and Sadock's Synopsis of Psychiatry, Eleventh Edition, Chapter 21st, Neurocognitive Disorders, Page no. 738.

Sample MCQ #4

After experiencing a road traffic accident in which she lost her brother, an 11-year-old girl presented with difficulty in sleep due to frightening and terrifying dreams involving the accident, which are prolonged and awaken her. On waking up, she remembers the dreams and finds herself sweating, panting and her heart racing. Due to such frequent fearful dreams, she often has fear of sleeping. Which of the following would best describe her condition?

ANight terrors
BNightmares
CHypnagogic hallucinations
DBoth A and B
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Nightmares

Nightmares are frightening or terrifying dreams , also called dream anxiety attacks, they produce sympathetic activation and ultimately awaken the dreamer. Nightmares occur in REM sleep and usually evolve from a long, complicated dream that becomes increasingly frightening. Nightmares have a strong association with traumatic events. Sympathetic over activity &rarr; sweating, palpitations, tachypnoea is seen in nightmares. Usually, patients can recollect the contents of the frightening dreams , which is how it can be differentiated from parasomnias of NREM/sleep like night terrors. Hypnagogic hallucinations are visual/auditory perceptions occurring while going to sleep. Reference: Kaplan and Sadock's Synopsis of Psychiatry, Eleventh edition, Chapter 16, Normal Sleep and Sleep-wake Disorders, Page no. 556.

Sample MCQ #5

Laughing or crying occurring spontaneously after seemingly minor provocation is observed in several patients who have suffered cerebrovascular stroke. This condition is known by all of the following terms except:

ACatastrophic reaction
BPathological laughter/ crying
CPseudobulbar effect
DEmotional incontinence
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Catastrophic reaction

Pathological laughter/ crying is defined as laughing and/or crying occurring spontaneously or after some seemingly minor provocation. Indicative of underlying depression. It is seen in post-stroke period. Other names: Emotional incontinence, emotional lability, pseudobulbar effect, pathological emotionalism, involuntary emotional expression disorder. Reference: Kaplan and Sadock&rsquo;s Comprehensive Textbook of Psychiatry, 10 th edition, Chapter 2.2 Neuropsychiatric aspects of cerebrovascular disorders, Page number 1283 .

Sample MCQ #6

A bibliophilic person, while reading a newspaper, may misread the word '-ook' as 'book' even though the faded letter is a 'l'. This kind of illusion is known as ___________.

ACompletion illusion
BAffect illusion
CPareidolia
DMuller-Lyer illusion
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Completion illusion

Completion illusion: It depends on inattention such as misreading words in newspapers or missing misprints because we read the word as if it were complete Alternatively, if we see faded letters, we may misread the word on the basis of our previous experience , our interests, etc. For example, to the person with an interest in reading, the word '-ook' might be misread as 'book' even though the faded letter was an 'l' Reference: Fish's Clinical Psychopathology, third edition, Chapter 3 Disorders of Perception, Page no. 26
