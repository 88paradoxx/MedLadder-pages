Sample MCQ #2

Who received Nobel Prize for demonstrating role of dopamine as a neurotransmitter in the brain?

AArvid Carlson
BCharpentier
CThomas Szasz
DDaniel Goleman
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Arvid Carlson

In 1957, Dr. Arvid Carlson showed that dopamine was a neurotransmitter in the brain and not just a precursor of norepinephrine. This was the prevailing view at that time. He also developed an assay to measure dopamine in the brain and found that the highest regional concentration existed in the basal ganglia. Reference: Arvid Carlson, and the story of dopamine (nih.gov)

Sample MCQ #5

What is the age range of juveniles who may be tried as adults for heinous offences under the JJ Act 2015?

A16-18 years
B15-17 years
C18-21 years
D14-16 years
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. 16-18 years

Explanation: Juveniles aged 16-18 years who commit heinous offences may be tried as adults under the Juvenile Justice Act of 2015, depending on the nature of the crime. Heinous offences are those punishable for a term of 7 years or more under the Indian Penal Code (IPC). Reference: The Juvenile Justice (Care and Protection of Children) Act, 2015

Sample MCQ #6

Which of the following best describes capacity in the context of informed consent?

AThe participant's ability to freely make decisions without external interference
BThe participant's ability to understand the information provided and form a reasonable judgment
CThe participant's understanding of the risks and benefits of the research
DThe participant's willingness to participate in the research study
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. The participant's ability to understand the information provided and form a reasonable judgment

Explanation: Informed consent is an autonomous decision made by an individual regarding medical interventions or research participation. It ensures individual autonomy and rational decision-making. Types of Consent: Informed vs. Real Consent: Informed: Full understanding of diagnosis, risks, and prognosis. Real: In line with the "prudent person rule" (reasonable doctor's judgment). Implicit vs. Explicit Consent: Implicit: Implied through actions (e.g., showing up for examination). Explicit: Clearly expressed, either verbal or written.

Sample MCQ #7

Which of the following noradrenergic receptors is presynaptic?

A&alpha;1
B&alpha;2
C&beta;1
D&beta;2
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. &alpha;2

All except one of the adrenergic receptors are postsynaptic. The only presynaptic adrenergic receptor is the &alpha;2-receptor (OPTION B IS CORRECT) . This is also known as a presynaptic auto-receptor. It is a G-protein couple receptor . Reference: Stahls Essential Psychopharmacology, Fourth edition, Chapter 2, Page no. 38.

Sample MCQ #8

Which of the following neurotransmitters does venlafaxine act on?

ADopamine only
BNoradrenaline only
CSerotonin and noradrenaline only
DSerotonin, noradrenaline and dopamine
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Serotonin, noradrenaline and dopamine

Venlafaxine has a unique mode of action. In low doses , it acts on the serotonin system only . As the dose increases , it acts on noradrenergic as well as serotonergic neurons. At higher does, i.e., 225 mg or above, it acts on all the three neurotransmitters, i.e. serotonin, noradrenaline and dopamine (OPTION D IS CORRECT) . In a nutshell, venlafaxine blocks the serotonin, noradrenaline and dopamine reuptake. In addition, blocking noradrenaline reuptake in the frontal cortex could increase dopamine neurotransmission in this part of brain. Reference: Stahls Essential Psychopharmacology, Fourth edition, Chapter 7th, Page no. 306.
