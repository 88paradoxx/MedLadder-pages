Sample MCQ #1

A 12-year-old girl complains of feeling depressed frequently and having nightmares since last 4 months. On delving deeper, she revealed to the psychiatrist that she has been having these symptoms after one of her uncles molested her in an isolated place. She has not talked about this event to anyone (including her parents) due to shame and requests the psychiatrist too not to reveal it to anyone. What should be the role of psychiatrist in this case?

AConfidentiality in this case can't be breached as the patient has denied revealing
BConfidentiality should be breached as this is a matter of child sexual abuse
CConfidentiality can be breached only after taking consent of the patient in this case
DConfidentiality should only be breached if the matter involves legal court
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Confidentiality should be breached as this is a matter of child sexual abuse

If a child has been the victim of physical or sexual abuse, then psychiatrists are required to make an immediate report to an appropriate agency . ( Option B is CORRECT ) In this situation, confidentiality is decisively limited by legal statute on the grounds that potential or actual harm to vulnerable children outweighs the value of confidentiality in a psychiatric setting. Reference : Kaplan and Sadock's Synopsis of Psychiatry, 11th edition, Chapter 36.1 Forensic Psychiatry, Page number 1383.

Sample MCQ #2

An expert psychiatrist has been called upon to assess testamentary capacity for a 65-year-old male contemplating on making a written will of his properties. Which of the following symptoms if present in this testator can suggest of testamentary incapacity?

ASeverely depressed mood
BParanoid delusions regarding his family members
CDelusion of poverty
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. All of the above

Symptoms of testamentary incapacity Difficulties in attention and information processing Language difficulties Memory difficulties Impairment of higher executive functions Persecutory delusions ( Option B ) Delusions of poverty( Option C ) Reference : https://www.lawyersclubindia.com/experts/files/61400_184055_article_on_wills.pdf

Sample MCQ #3

Which statements are correct with respect to Mental Healthcare Act 2017 ? 1. Suicidal act has been decriminalised 2. Advanced directive and Nominated representative are concepts newly introduced 3. ECT has been approved for use in minors 4. No restrictions on performing Psychosurgery 5. Chaining in any manner or form is prohibited 6. ECT can be used as a form of emergency treatment

AStatements 1, 4, 6 are correct
BStatements 2, 4, 5 are correct
CStatements 1, 2, 5 are correct
DStatements 2, 3, 4 are correct
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Statements 1, 2, 5 are correct

According to Mental healthcare Act 2017, Section 309 suicide attempt is decriminalised , considering the fact that those attempting suicide undergo lot of stress ( Statement 1 is Correct ) Advanced directive which allows a person to modify one&rsquo;s treatment options and Nominated representative can be appointed. ( Statement 2 is Correct ) Use of ECT in minors is restricted; if needed, permission from Mental Health Review Board is essential. Also, ECT cannot be used as emergency treatment. (Statement 3 & 6 are Wrong) Similarly, performing psychosurgeries like lobotomies or cingulotomy is restricted without permission from Mental Health Review Board. (Statement 4 is Wrong) Chaining (physical restraint) is prohibited procedure under this act. (Statement 5 is Correct) Reference : https://www.prsindia.org/uploads/media/Mental%20Health/Mental%20Healthcare%20Act,%202017.pdf
