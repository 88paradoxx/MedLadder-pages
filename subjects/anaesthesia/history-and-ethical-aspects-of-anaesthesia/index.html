Sample MCQ #1

Who coined the term "balanced anaesthesia"?

AOliver holmes
BJohn snow
CWTG morton
DJohn lundy
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. John lundy

Oliver Wendel Holmes coined the term Anaesthesia . John snow- administered chloroform analgesia to Queen Victoria for the delivery of her eighth child. This was a success and led to medical and religious acceptance of obstetrical anesthesia. When cholera returned to London in 1853, John Snow undertook an epidemiological study and linked the outbreak in Golden Square to the water pump on Broad Street. WTG Morton is considered as father of Anaesthesia The concept of balanced anesthesia was introduced in 1926 by Lundy and others and evolved to include thiopental for induction, nitrous oxide for amnesia, an opioid for analgesia, and curare for muscle relaxation. The term "spinal anesthesia " was coined in 1885 by Leonard Corning, a neurologist,and Augest Bier used cocaine for spinal anesthesia Reference : Lundy Js . Balanced Anaesthesia .minn .med . 1926;9:399-404

Sample MCQ #2

World Anaesthesia Day is celebrated on ?

A16 December
B16 October
C16 November
D16 September
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. 16 October

In the Bullfinch amphitheater of the Massachusetts General Hospital. On Friday 16 October 1846 , Morton administed volatile anesthetic Ether to Edward Gilbert Abbott when John Collins Warren was operating a vascular lesion at the left side of Abbott's neck Gilbert Abbott later reported that he was aware of the operation but had experienced no pain . At the moment that the procedure ended, Warren turned to his audience and allegedly announced, "Gentlemen, this is no humbug" To honor this humongous achievement, the day is celebrated as world anaesthesia day Reference : E Book of Baheti's Understanding Anesthetist Equipments and Procedures a Pratical Approach First Edition Chapter 1 Page No 4 Table 2

Sample MCQ #3

"Chloroform a lareine", a technique was invented by ?

AJames Young Simpson
BJohn Snow
CJoseph Clover
DJoseph lister
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. John Snow

John snow administered labour analgesia using Chloroform to Queen Victoria's 8 th delivery, to the birth of, Prince Leopard During the delivery, Snow administered analgesic doses of chloroform on a folded handkerchief, a technique that was soon termed ' chloroform a la reine ' Victoria abhorred the pain of labor and enjoyed the relief that chloroform provided; she wrote in her journal," Dr.Snow gave that blessed chloroform and the effect was soothing, quieting, and delightful beyond measure " Reference : E Book of Baheti's Understanding Anesthetist Equipments and Procedures a Pratical Approach First Edition Chapter 1 Page No 4 Table 2

Sample MCQ #4

For elective procedures, an anesthesia provider must obtain informed and preferably written consent

AJust prior to transferring the patient to the operation room for surgery
BDuring preoperative anesthetic evaluation
CAt the same time that a surgeon obtains consent for the surgical procedure
DJust prior to induction of anesthesia in the operating room
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. During preoperative anesthetic evaluation

An anesthesia consent should be obtained during pre-anesthetic evaluation, whenever possible. This is one of the prime aims that need to be filled as a component of preoperative anesthetic evaluation. Reference: Morgan and Mikhail's clinical anesthesiology 6th edition pg 297.
