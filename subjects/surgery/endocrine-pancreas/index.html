Sample MCQ #1

Which among the following is not a complication of bladder drainage of exocrine pancreas?

ABladder anastomotic leak
BCystitis
CReflux pancreatitis
DMetabolic alkalosis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Metabolic alkalosis

Bladder drainage of the exocrine pancreas may result in the following complications - Bladder/duodenal anastomotic leaks Cystitis (owing to effect of pancreatic enzymes) Urethritis/urethral stricture Reflux pancreatitis Urinary tract infection Haematuria Metabolic acidosis (due to loss of bicarbonate in the urine). Reference : Bailey & Love's short practice of surgery 27th Edition, Chapter 82, Transplantation, Pg: 1553.

Sample MCQ #2

Most common pituitary tumour in clinical practice is:

AGH producing adenoma
BACTH producing adenoma
CFunctional adenoma
DNon-functional adenoma
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Functional adenoma

Explanation: Among pituitary adenomas that present clinically, prolactin-secreting (prolactinomas) are the most common functioning tumours. Functioning adenomas (especially prolactinomas) produce hormonal syndromes that bring patients to clinical attention earlier. Non-functioning adenomas are also common (often present as macroadenomas with mass effect), but prolactinomas are the most common single subtype encountered in clinical practice. Explanation of other options: GH producing adenomas (Option A) : causes acromegaly; are less common than prolactinomas. ACTH adenomas (Option B ): causes Cushing's disease and are less common. Non-functional adenomas (Option D) : are common (often diagnosed by mass effect), but among clinical presenting tumours with endocrine features , functioning adenomas (especially prolactinomas) are the most common single type.

Zollinger-Ellison syndrome (ZES)/gastrinoma is a condition that includes: (1) fulminating ulcer or atypical sites; (2) recurrent ulceration despite 'adequate' therapy; and (3) non-beta islet cell tumours of the pancreas (gastrinoma). Sample MCQ #7

VIPoma is associated with which syndrome:

AZollinger Ellison
BVerner Morrison
CCarcinoid syndrome
DCushing's syndrome
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Verner Morrison

Ans is 'b' i.e. Verner Morrison VlPomas are endocrine tumors that secrete excessive amounts of vasoactive intestinal peptide (VIP), Excessive VIP causes a distinct syndrome characterized by large- volume diarrhea, hypokalemia, and dehydration. This syndrome also is called Verner-Morrison syndrome, pancreatic cholera, and WDHA syndrome for watery diarrhea, hypokalemia, and achlorhydria. The principal symptoms are large-volume diarrhea (100%) severe enough to cause hypokalemia (80-100%), dehydration (83%), hypochlorhydria (54-76%), and flushing (20%).
