Sample MCQ #3

Which is the most common site of occurrence of ductal adenocarcinoma?

AHead of pancreas
BNeck of pancreas
CBody of pancreas
DTail of pancreas
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Head of pancreas

Ductal adenocarcinomas - Arise most commonly in the head of the pancreas . They are solid, scirrhous tumours, characterised by neoplastic tubular glands within a markedly desmoplastic fibrous stroma . Infiltrate locally , typically along nerve sheaths, along lymphatics and into blood vessels. Liver and peritoneal metastases are common. Proliferative lesions in the pancreatic ducts can precede invasive ductal adenocarcinoma. These are termed pancreatic intraepithelial neoplasia (PanIN), and can demonstrate a range of structural complexity and cellular atypia. Reference : Bailey & Love's short practice of surgery, 28th Edition, Chapter 72, The Pancreas, Pg: 1281
