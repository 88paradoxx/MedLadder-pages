Sample MCQ #1

A patient presented with painless hard enlargement of the testis. LDH values are increased. Next best step is?

AFNAC
BBiopsy
CHigh inguinal orchidectomy
DTumor markers
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. High inguinal orchidectomy

The c/f and the raised AFP levels are suggestive of a testicular tumor, probably a non-seminomatous germ cell tumor (NSGCT) and the next best step in management is high inguinal orchidectomy. Painless testicular enlargement: Testicular Tumor Syphilis Testicular tumors are of: Seminoma: most common type, with normal alpha fetoprotein and HCG, and mild elevation of LDH Non seminomatous: very high levels of alpha fetoprotein and HCG Transcrotal biopsy is contraindicated as it causes dissemination of tumor cells Next step in case of testicular tumor - chevassu's manuvere where testis is delivered by high inguinal orchidectomy

Sample MCQ #2

A young male was found to have a testicular swelling. After evaluation- HIGH INGUINAL ORCHIDECTOMY was done. What will be the probable diagnosis?

ATorsion
BSeminoma testis
CFournier gangrene
DHematocele
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Seminoma testis

HIGH INGUINAL ORCHIDECTOMY is done for seminoma of testis or any other testicular tumor, because if operated via scrotum there is increased risk of dissemination of cancer cells. Option a) torsion testis- orchidopexy is done if the testis is viable Option c) fourniers gangrene: testis not affected in this condition, so ORCHIDECTOMY is not done Option d) hematocele initially aspiration is done, if recurrent excision of the cyst is done

Sample MCQ #3

A 14 years old boy is presented with absent left testis in scrotum. On examination, left testis was found in the superior inguinal pouch. What is the diagnosis?

ACryptorchidism
BLeft undescended testis
CLeft ectopic testis
DRetractile testis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Left ectopic testis

Ectopic testis: an ectopic testis is one that has ended up away from the normal path of descent. The commonest site is the superfcial inguinal pouch, just inferior and medial to the superfcial inguinal ring. Other rarer ectopic sites include the femoral triangle, root of the penis and perineum

Sample MCQ #4 · Image-based

A 60-year-old man with a known case of diabetes, presented to surgical OPD with swollen blackened Scrotum as shown below in the picture. All of the following statements about the condition are correct except:

AIt is also known as synergistic spreading gangrene
BSubdermal spread is faster than superficial spread
CPain, Gangrene, crepitus and smell are characteristic of this condition
DRemoval of scrotum with testes must be done to clear infection
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Removal of scrotum with testes must be done to clear infection

Option A: The condition in the picture shown is that of Fournier gangrene. It is more common in diabetic and immunosuppressive patients. It occurs due to polymicrobial infection in which aerobic bacteria destroy living tissue allowing anerobic bacteria to thrive.

Option B: It affects subcutaneous tissue more, so it spreads fast in subdermal space. Thus, during debridement, wide excision of tissue more than skin is done to remove infection which is more likely to spread in subdermal space than skin.

Option C: Severe wound pain, signs of spreading inflammation with crepitus, gangrene and smell are characteristic of spreading Fournier gangrene.

Option D: Testes should NOT be removed as Fournier affects scrotum not testes. In case of inefficient vascularity or spreading to testes it would have to be removed

Reference: Bailey & Love's short practice of surgery, chapter: 5, Edition: 28th, Pg: 58
