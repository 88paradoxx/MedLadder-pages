Sample MCQ #1

Best diagnostic method for parathyroid adenoma is

ASestamibi SPECT
BTc99m sestamibi scan
CUSG neck
DPET/CT
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. PET/CT

Preference investigations for parathyroid diseases in terms of reliablity ( from best to least ) PET - CT - MOST RELIABLE Sestamibi SPECT - better than Tc99m sestamibi scan Tech 99m - Sestamibi scan- most commonly used USG - LEAST RELIABLE The sensitivity of sestamibi SPECT is 79% for Parathyroid adenoma. The sensitivity of PET\CT is 93 % for Parathyroid adenoma.

Sample MCQ #2

A 45 year old female patient on examination of her breast a 4.5cm * 3cm size irregular mass is felt in the left upper quadrant of the right breast which is not fixed to the pectoralis major muscle and no skin changes, a 2cm*1.5cm lymph node is also palpated in the right axilla. No mass felt in the opposite breast. What all investigations will you do to this patient? 1. Mammography 2. PET scan 3. Biopsy of the breast mass 4. USG guided biopsy of the enlarged axillary lymph node

A1,2,3,4
B1,3
C1,2,3
D1,3,4
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. 1,3

According to the case scenario-staging of breast carcinoma - T2N1 - early breast cancer Mammography in two planes and ultrasonography are the first line investigations for imaging the breast, it is done for both the breast and also to look for multicnetricity PET scan is done for locally advanced breast cancer TRU CUT biopsy of lump is the gold standard investigation as it is a tissue diagnosis USG guided biopsy of the enlarged axillary lymph node is of no use as, if axillary lymph node are enlarged itself indicates local metastasis, which should be managed by axillary dissection.
