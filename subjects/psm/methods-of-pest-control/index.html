Sample MCQ #1

Best approach to control of arthropods is

AEnvironmental control
BChemical control
CBiological control
DGenetic control
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Environmental control

The correct answer is A. Environmental Control

The best approach to control arthropods is environmental control, which involves modifying the environment to make it less conducive to arthropod breeding and survival.

A. Environmental Control:

  • Environmental control methods focus on eliminating or reducing the factors that favor arthropod proliferation, such as removing standing water (which serves as breeding sites for mosquitoes), sealing cracks and crevices in buildings to prevent entry, and proper waste management to reduce food sources for pests. This approach is considered sustainable and environmentally friendly because it minimizes reliance on chemical pesticides and can lead to long-term suppression of arthropod populations.

Why the other options are not suitable:

B. Chemical Control:

  • Chemical control involves the use of pesticides to kill or repel arthropods. While chemical pesticides can be effective in reducing arthropod populations, they may also have adverse effects on non-target organisms, including humans, pets, and beneficial insects. Additionally, overuse or misuse of chemical pesticides can lead to the development of resistance in arthropod populations, making them less effective over time.

C. Biological Control:

  • Biological control utilizes natural enemies of arthropods, such as predators, parasitoids, and pathogens, to regulate their populations. This approach can be effective and environmentally sustainable when implemented correctly. However, it often requires careful selection and introduction of biological control agents, as well as monitoring to ensure they do not harm non-target species.

D. Genetic Control:

  • Genetic control methods involve the manipulation of arthropod genetics to reduce their reproductive capacity or enhance susceptibility to control measures. While genetic control holds promise for targeted pest management, it is still an emerging field with practical challenges and ethical considerations.

In summary, while chemical, biological, and genetic control methods have their uses in arthropod management, environmental control is often considered the best approach because it focuses on addressing the root causes of pest infestations while minimizing negative impacts on the environment and non-target organisms

Sample MCQ #2

Organophosphate insecticides are all except

AA, D, E
BB, D, E
CAll of the above
DNone of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. A, D, E

The correct answer is A, D & E.

Organophosphate insecticides are a class of chemicals used to control pests like insects and mites. They work by disrupting the nervous system of these organisms.

A. Dieldrin:

  • Dieldrin is not an organophosphate insecticide; it belongs to the class of chemicals known as cyclodiene insecticides. It was widely used in the past for controlling soil-dwelling and crop pests. However, due to its persistence in the environment and harmful effects on non-target organisms, including humans, wildlife, and aquatic life, its use has been heavily restricted or banned in many countries.

B. Fenthion:

  • Fenthion is indeed an organophosphate insecticide. It is commonly used to control a wide range of pests in agriculture, public health, and veterinary applications. It acts by inhibiting the activity of acetylcholinesterase, an enzyme crucial for nerve function in insects.

C. Diazinon:

  • Diazinon is also an organophosphate insecticide. It was widely used in agriculture, as well as for residential pest control, but its use has been significantly restricted or phased out in many countries due to concerns about its toxicity to humans, wildlife, and the environment.

D. Propoxur:

  • Propoxur, also known as Baygon, is not an organophosphate insecticide; it belongs to the carbamate class of insecticides. Like organophosphates, carbamates act on the nervous system of insects but through a different mechanism. Propoxur is commonly used for indoor pest control, such as against cockroaches, ants, and fleas.

E. Lindane:

  • Lindane, also known as gamma-hexachlorocyclohexane (γ-HCH), is not an organophosphate insecticide; it is a chlorinated hydrocarbon insecticide. It was widely used for agricultural and medical purposes, including as a pesticide for controlling pests like lice and scabies. However, due to its persistence, bioaccumulation, and toxicity concerns, its use has been restricted or banned in many countries.

In summary, while fenthion and diazinon are organophosphate insecticides, dieldrin, propoxur, and lindane belong to different classes of insecticides.

Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following about DDT is false

APyrethrum has synergistic action
BIt is a contact poison
CImmediately kills the prey
DResidual effect lasts 18 months
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Immediately kills the prey

Option C, "DDT immediately kills the prey," is false. DDT (dichlorodiphenyltrichloroethane) is not known for its immediate lethal effect on insects upon contact.

C. Immediately kills the prey (False):

  • DDT does not immediately kill insects upon contact. Instead, it typically takes some time for the insecticide to affect the nervous system of the insect, leading to paralysis and eventual death. The onset of action may vary depending on factors such as the concentration of DDT, the species of insect, and environmental conditions.

Why the other options are not suitable:

A. Pyrethrum has synergistic action:

  • Pyrethrum is a natural insecticide derived from chrysanthemum flowers. When combined with DDT, it exhibits synergistic action, meaning that the combined effect of the two compounds is greater than the sum of their individual effects. Pyrethrum can enhance the insecticidal properties of DDT, making it more effective against a broader range of pests.

B. It is a contact poison:

  • DDT primarily acts as a contact poison, meaning that it kills insects upon contact with their bodies. It disrupts the nervous system of insects, leading to paralysis and eventual death. DDT is not typically ingested by insects but rather absorbed through their exoskeletons when they come into direct contact with treated surfaces.

D. Residual effect lasts 18 months:

  • DDT is known for its long-lasting residual effect. After application to surfaces, DDT can persist for an extended period, providing continued protection against insect pests. The residual effect of DDT can last for up to 18 months or even longer under favorable conditions, making it effective for controlling insect populations over an extended period.

In summary, while options A, B, and D accurately describe characteristics of DDT, option C is false because DDT does not immediately kill insects upon contact. Instead, it takes some time for the insecticide to exert its toxic effects on the nervous system of the target pests.

Reference: Park's Preventive and social medicine, 27th EDITION Pg 909

Sample MCQ #4

Imidacloprid is used for control of

AHouseflies
BCockroaches
CBoth
DNone
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Both

Imidacloprid is a widely used insecticide that belongs to the neonicotinoid class of chemicals. It is primarily used for the control of various insect pests in agricultural, residential, and commercial settings.

a. Houseflies:

  • Imidacloprid is not commonly used specifically for the control of houseflies. While it may have some efficacy against houseflies, it is not the primary insecticide of choice for controlling this particular pest. Houseflies are typically managed using other insecticides or integrated pest management (IPM) strategies that target their breeding sites and behaviour.

b. Cockroaches:

  • Imidacloprid is not typically used for the control of cockroaches. Cockroach control often involves the use of insecticides specifically formulated for targeting these resilient pests, such as baits, sprays, or dusts that are effective against cockroach infestations. Imidacloprid may not provide the most effective control of cockroaches compared to other insecticides that are more commonly used for this purpose.

c. Both (Correct):

  • Imidacloprid is indeed used for the control of various insect pests, including both houseflies and cockroaches, among others. It has broad-spectrum activity against a wide range of insect pests, making it suitable for use in various environments such as agricultural fields, homes, and commercial buildings. Imidacloprid works by interfering with the transmission of nerve impulses in insects, leading to paralysis and eventual death.

d. None:

  • This option is incorrect because imidacloprid is indeed used for pest control purposes, as explained in option C. It is an effective insecticide commonly employed by pest control professionals, farmers, and homeowners for managing insect infestations.

In summary, option C is the correct answer because imidacloprid is used for the control of both houseflies and cockroaches, as well as other insect pests, due to its broad-spectrum activity and effectiveness in insect control.
