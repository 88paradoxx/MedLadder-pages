Sample MCQ #1

There is an outbreak of encephalitis in school children due to lack of proper childhood vaccination. According to universal immunization schedule, Which of the following type and route of vaccine that could have prevented this

ALive and subcutaneous
BKilled and subcutaneous
CLive and intramuscular
DKilled and intramuscular
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Killed and intramuscular

JENVAC is a single dose inactivated Japanese Encephalitis (JE) Vaccine. This Vero cell derived vaccine is prepared from an Indian strain (Kolar- 821564XY) of the JE virus. JENVAC has been developed in collaboration with the National Institute of Virology, India. It is a safe and highly-effective vaccine that protects against all known strains of Japanese Encephalitis. Data of 2-dose study shows that a single dose of JENVAC is sufficient to elicit the immune response as the subjects who received a single dose were 98.67% sero-protected and the 4-fold sero-conversion was at 93.14% for the &ge;1 year to &le;50 years age group. Reference - Park's Preventive and social medicine, 27th EDITION Pg 111

Sample MCQ #2 · Image-based

Which disease the vector in picture causes?

ALyme's disease
BCarrion's disease
CBabesiosis
DJapanese encephalitis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Carrion's disease

The correct answer is b. Carrion's disease

Phlebotomine sand flies are vectors for various diseases, including Carrion's disease, which is caused by the bacterium Bartonella bacilliformis. This disease is primarily found in the Andean valleys of Peru, Ecuador, and Colombia. The sand flies transmit the bacteria to humans through their bite, leading to two distinct phases of the disease: Oroya fever (acute phase) and verruga peruana (chronic phase).

Why the other options are not suitable:

a. Lyme's disease:

  • Lyme disease is primarily transmitted to humans through the bite of infected black-legged ticks (also known as deer ticks), not by phlebotomine sand flies.

c. Babesiosis:

  • Babesiosis is a malaria-like illness caused by protozoan parasites of the genus Babesia, which are transmitted primarily by ticks, not by sand flies.

d. Japanese encephalitis:

  • Japanese encephalitis is a viral infection transmitted by mosquitoes, particularly those of the Culex species, and is not transmitted by sand flies.

Reference -Park's Preventive and social medicine, 27th EDITION Pg 902
