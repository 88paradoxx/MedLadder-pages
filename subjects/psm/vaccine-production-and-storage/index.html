About Vaccine Production and Storage MCQs

Vaccine Production and Storage MCQs ask which vaccines fall outside the open vial policy — measles among them — and how storage conditions protect potency, making this a compact but high-yield NEET PG topic.

Sample Vaccine Production and Storage MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

Which of these vaccines are not covered under the open vial policy?

AOPV
BDPT
CTT
DMeasles
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Measles

Explanation: The measles vaccine does not follow the open vial policy. The open vial policy states that vaccine vials, once opened, can be used for multiple sessions over a period of up to 28 days, as long as they are kept under proper cold chain conditions to minimize vaccine wastage. This policy applies to all liquid vaccines, such as Hepatitis B , DPT, Pentavalent, OPV, IPV, PCV, and Td. However, reconstituted vaccines like MMR, BCG, JE , Yellow Fever, and Rotavirus vaccines do not follow the open vial policy.

Sample MCQ #2 · Image-based

Which all vaccine vial monitors shown below can be used

AA & B can be used
BA & C can be used
CB & D can be used
DC & D can be used
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. A & B can be used

Vaccine vial monitors (VVMs) are labels affixed to vaccine vials to indicate whether the vaccine has been exposed to heat or has lost its potency. They help healthcare workers determine whether a vaccine is safe to use, especially in areas with unreliable access to refrigeration. The stages of vaccine vial monitors typically include the following:

A. Stage 1: The inner square is lighter than the outer circle. If the expiry date has not passed, USE the vaccine.

B. Stage 2: As time passes the inner square is still lighter than the outer circle. If the expiry date has not passed, USE the vaccine.

C. Stage 3: Discard point: the color of the inner square matches that of the outer circle. DO NOT USE the vaccine.

D. Stage 4: Beyond the discard point: inner square is darker than the outer circle. DO NOT USE the vaccine.

Overall, vaccine vial monitors provide visual cues to healthcare workers about the integrity of vaccines, helping them make informed decisions about whether to administer the vaccine based on its exposure to heat or cold.
