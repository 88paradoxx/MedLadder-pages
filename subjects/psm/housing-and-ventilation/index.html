Sample MCQ #1

The ideal per capita space for students in a classroom should be:

A>5 sq. ft.
B>10 sq. ft.
C>15 sq. ft.
D>20 sq. ft.
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. >10 sq. ft.

Explanation: The ideal per capita space in a classroom is recommended to be greater than 10 sq. ft. per student. (Options A, C & D ruled out) This ensures students have sufficient room to sit comfortably, move slightly, and participate in activities. Adequate space reduces overcrowding, promotes ventilation, and improves attention and learning outcomes.
