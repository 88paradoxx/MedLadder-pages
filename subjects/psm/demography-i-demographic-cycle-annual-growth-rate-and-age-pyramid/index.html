Sample MCQ #2

Transition from increased prevalence of infectious pandemic diseases to man made disease is known as

AParadoxical transition
BReversal of transition
CEpidemiological transition
DDemographic transition
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Epidemiological transition

Epidemiologic transition is the process by which the pattern of mortality and disease in a population is transformed from high mortality among infants and children and episodic famine and epidemics affecting all age groups to one of degenerative and human-made diseases such as those attributed to human behavior affecting principally the elderly eg: Life style diseases. Reference: Park's Preventive and social medicine, 27th Edition, Chapter Concept Of Health & Disease.

Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following statements about 'Late Expanding Phase of Demographic Cycle' is TRUE?

ABirth Rate is lower than Death Rate
BHigh Death Rate and High Birth Rate
CDeath Rate declines more than Birth Rate
DDeath Rate begins to decline while Birth Rate remains unchanged
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Death Rate declines more than Birth Rate

In late expanding phase of demographic cycle death rates declines faster than the birth rate and there is a steady decrease in the demographic gap. In this state population grow at a steadily decreasing rate. Most of the developing countries are now at early expanding and late expanding stages of demographic cycle.
