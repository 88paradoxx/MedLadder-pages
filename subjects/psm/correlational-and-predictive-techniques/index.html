Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following is false regarding Correlation coefficient:

AIs denoted by the symbol "r"
BDescribes the degree of agreement between two variables
CA positive value implies that a rise in one variable accompanies a rise in the other.
DDescribes the degree of association between two variables
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Describes the degree of agreement between two variables

b) Describes the degree of agreement between two variables:

This statement is false. The correlation coefficient measures the degree of association or relationship between two variables, not the degree of agreement. Agreement typically refers to the similarity or consistency between two measurements or observations, which is assessed using different statistical measures such as agreement coefficients or concordance correlation coefficients.

Explanation as to why the other options are not correct:

a) Is denoted by the symbol "r":

This statement is true. The correlation coefficient is commonly denoted by the symbol "r".

c) A positive value implies that a rise in one variable accompanies a rise in the other:

- This statement is true. A positive correlation coefficient indicates that as one variable increases, the other variable tends to increase as well, and vice versa.

d) Describes the degree of association between two variables:

This statement is true. The correlation coefficient measures the strength and direction of the linear relationship between two variables. It describes how closely the two variables are related.

In summary, option e is false regarding the correlation coefficient.

Reference: Park's Preventive and social medicine, 27th EDITION Pg 980

Sample MCQ #2

Which of the following is not true about correlation

AIt indicates degree of association between 2 characteristics
BCorrelation coefficient of 1 means that two variables exhibit linear relationship
CCorrelation can measure risk
DCausation implies correlation
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Correlation can measure risk

Correlation measures the degree and direction of the association between two variables. It quantifies how one variable change when the other variable changes. However, correlation does not measure risk. Risk is typically assessed using measures such as relative risk, odds ratio, or hazard ratio, depending on the context of the study.

Explanation as to why the other options are not correct:

a) It indicates the degree of association between 2 characteristics.

This statement is true. Correlation measures the strength and direction of the relationship between two variables.

b) Correlation coefficient of 1 means that two variables exhibit a linear relationship.

This statement is true. A correlation coefficient of +1 indicates a perfect positive linear relationship, where an increase in one variable corresponds to a proportional increase in the other variable. Similarly, a correlation coefficient of -1 indicates a perfect negative linear relationship.

d) Causation implies correlation.

This statement is not universally true. While correlation can suggest a potential relationship between variables, it does not imply causation. Correlation only measures the association between variables, but it does not prove that changes in one variable cause changes in another variable. Causation requires additional evidence from experimental studies or rigorous observational studies with appropriate control for confounding factors.

Reference: Park's Preventive and social medicine, 27th EDITION Pg 980

Sample MCQ #3

The correlation between Smoking and Lung cancer in a study was found to be 1.5. This indicates

AVery strong correlation
BModerately strong correlation
CWeak correlation
DComputational mistakes in calculation
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Computational mistakes in calculation

The correlation coefficient r measures the strength and direction of the linear relationship between two variables. It ranges from -1 to 1, where:

r = 1 indicates a perfect positive linear relationship,

r = -1 indicates a perfect negative linear relationship,

r = 0 indicates no linear relationship.

Given that the correlation between smoking and lung cancer was found to be 1.5, which is outside the valid range of -1 to 1, it indicates a computational mistake in the calculation rather than a meaningful correlation value. Therefore, option D is correct.

Explanation as to why the other options are not correct:

a) Very strong correlation

This option would be incorrect even if the value was within the valid range of -1 to 1, as 1.5 would suggest a correlation stronger than perfect, which is not possible.

b) Moderately strong correlation

Similar to option a, this option would also be incorrect even if the value was within the valid range, as 1.5 would suggest a correlation stronger than perfect.

c) Weak correlation

This option would be incorrect if the value was within the valid range, as 1.5 would indicate a strong positive correlation, not a weak one. However, since the value is outside the valid range, the main issue is the computational mistake rather than the strength of the correlation.

Reference: Park's Preventive and social medicine, 27th EDITION Pg 980

Aa, c
Bb, d

Da, b, c, d

  2. While r2 = 0.52 indicates that 52% of the variability in systolic blood pressure can be explained by its linear relationship with age, this statement is not explicitly supported by the given data. The coefficient of determination r2 represents the proportion of the variance in one variable that is predictable from the other variable. It does not imply causation or explain the actual percentage of variability explained.

Sample MCQ #5

Which is the best method to compare the results obtained by a new test and gold standard test

ACorrelational study
BRegression study
CBland and Altman analysis
DKolmogorov- Smirnov test
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Bland and Altman analysis

When comparing the results obtained by a new test with those of a gold standard test, it is crucial to assess the agreement between the two methods. Bland and Altman analysis, also known as the Bland-Altman plot or method, is a widely used technique for this purpose.

Bland and Altman analysis provides a comprehensive assessment of agreement by plotting the differences between measurements obtained by the two methods against their means. The plot allows visual inspection of any systematic bias or proportional error between the methods and helps identify outliers or areas of poor agreement. Additionally, summary statistics such as the mean difference and limits of agreement can quantify the level of agreement between the two methods.

Explanation as to why the other options are not correct:

a) Correlational study: While correlation analysis can provide insights into the relationship between the results of two tests, it does not specifically address the agreement between the two methods. Correlation coefficients indicate the strength and direction of the linear relationship between variables but do not assess the agreement in measurement values between the new test and the gold standard.

b) Regression study: Regression analysis can be used to model the relationship between variables and predict one variable based on another. However, it does not directly assess the agreement between the results obtained by the new test and the gold standard. Regression analysis focuses more on understanding the association between variables rather than evaluating agreement.

d) Kolmogorov-Smirnov test: The Kolmogorov-Smirnov test is a nonparametric test used to compare the distributions of two samples. It assesses whether two samples are drawn from the same distribution. While it can be useful in some contexts, it does not specifically evaluate the agreement between measurements obtained by two different methods.

In summary, Bland and Altman analysis is the preferred method for comparing the results obtained by a new test with those of a gold standard test because it specifically evaluates agreement and provides valuable insights into the accuracy and reliability of the new test compared to the gold standard.

Reference - Park’s Preventive and social medicine, 27th EDITION, Chapter Bio-Statistics.

Sample MCQ #6

All of the following statements are true about the slope of the linear regression line between an explanatory variable, x, and a dependent variable, y, except

AThe same as the gradient of the line.
BThe value of Y when x = 0, where Y is the predicted value of y.
CThe average change in Y for a unit increase in x.
DOften called the regression coefficient
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. The value of Y when x = 0, where Y is the predicted value of y.

b) The value of Y when x = 0 is not necessarily equal to the slope of the regression line. In linear regression, the predicted value of y when x = 0 is known as the y-intercept, which is a separate parameter estimated by the regression model.

Explanation as to why the other options are not correct:

a) The slope of the linear regression line represents the change in the dependent variable (y) for a one-unit change in the independent variable (x). It is indeed the same as the gradient of the line.

c) The slope of the regression line also represents the average change in the dependent variable (y) for a one-unit increase in the independent variable (x). It quantifies the linear relationship between the two variables.

d) The slope of the regression line is indeed often called the regression coefficient. It reflects the extent to which the dependent variable (y) changes when the independent variable (x) changes by one unit, assuming a linear relationship between them.

Reference: Park's Preventive and social medicine, 27th EDITION Pg 981
