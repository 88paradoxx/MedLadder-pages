Sample MCQ #3

To find out the incidence of deafness in factory workers, the suitable study design is

ACross sectional
BCase control
CCohort
DRCT
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Cohort

ANALYSIS OF COHORT STUDY The data are analyzed in terms of : a.Incidence rates of outcome among exposed and non exposed, b.Estimation of risk. INCIDENCE RATES In a cohort study, we can determine incidence rates directly in those exposed and those not exposed. Estimation of risk Estimation of risk of outcome (e.g., disease or death) in the exposed and non-exposed cohorts is done in terms of two well-known indices: (a) relative risk, (b) attributable risk.

BDescriptive epidemiology

✓ Correct Answer: B. Descriptive epidemiology

Formulation of hypothesis is associated with Descriptive epidemiology eg: doing survey in cross sectional study Testing of hypothesis is associated with Analytic epidemiology- eg Doing case control and cohort Reference: Park's Preventive and social medicine, 27th edition, Chapter Principles of Epidemiology & Epidemiology methods.
