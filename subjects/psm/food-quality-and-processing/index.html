Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following is/are true regarding lathyrism

AVitamin C prophylaxis
BParboiling detoxicates pulses
CBOAA is the causative toxin
DAll the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. All the above

a. Vitamin C prophylaxis: Vitamin C prophylaxis has been suggested as a preventive measure against lathyrism. Vitamin C may help mitigate the toxic effects of BOAA by enhancing the metabolism and excretion of the neurotoxin. b. Parboiling detoxicates pulses: Parboiling, or partially cooking, pulses (such as chickpeas or lentils) has been observed to reduce the concentration of BOAA and render them safer for consumption. Parboiling helps to leach out the neurotoxic compounds, including BOAA, thus reducing the risk of lathyrism. c. BOAA is the causative toxin : BOAA is indeed the causative toxin responsible for lathyrism. When consumed in large quantities over an extended period, BOAA can lead to the development of lathyrism, characterized by progressive flaccid paralysis and other neurological symptoms. Reference: Park's Preventive and social medicine, 27th EDITION Pg 759

Sample MCQ #4 · Image-based

Identify the organization represented by the logo shown below.

AAGMARK
BBureau of Indian Standards
CFood Corporation of India
DGST
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Bureau of Indian Standards

Explanation:

The logo depicted belongs to the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS). As the national standards body of India, it operates under the Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution.

  • Role: The BIS is responsible for the harmonious development of standardization, marking, and quality certification of goods in India. Its standards ensure quality and safety for consumers.
  • The Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI), under the Ministry of Health, sets mandatory food standards, often adopting guidelines from the Codex Alimentarius.
  • AGMARK is a certification mark for agricultural products, administered by the Ministry of Agriculture.

Explanation of other options:
AGMARK (Option A): This is a certification mark for agricultural produce, not the national standards body for general goods and services.

Food Corporation of India (Option C): This is a central government entity responsible for the procurement, storage, and distribution of food grains, not for setting general quality standards.

GST (Option D): This stands for Goods and Services Tax, a form of indirect tax, and is not an organization or a standards body.

Key Takeaways:

  • BIS is the national standards body of India.
  • FSSAI is the apex food regulator.
  • AGMARK certifies the quality of agricultural products
