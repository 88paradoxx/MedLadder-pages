Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following drugs are used for clinical cure of malaria? Artemether Sulfadoxine-pyrimethamine Chloroquine Primaquine

A1, 2
B1, 2, 3
C3, 4
D1, 2, 4
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. 1, 2, 3

Explanation: Clinical cure refers to the treatment that clears the parasite burden from the blood, relieving the acute symptoms of malaria. This is achieved by schizonticidal drugs . Artemether (an artemisinin derivative), Sulfadoxine-pyrimethamine (a sulfa drug combination), and Chloroquine are all blood schizonticides and are used for clinical cure. Primaquine is a tissue schizonticide (acts on hypnozoites in the liver for P. vivax and P. ovale) and a gametocidal It is not used for clinical cure of the acute attack but for radical cure (preventing relapse) and preventing transmission. Key Takeaways: Blood schizonticides (e.g., Chloroquine, Artemisinin, SP) are for clinical cure. Primaquine is for radical cure and gametocidal action.

Sample MCQ #2

In a town with population of 1,00,000, the number of slides examined is 50,000. Out of these, 100 slides were positive for malaria. The API is-

A0.1
B0.5
C1
D2
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. 1

The Annual Parasite Index (API) is a measure used to assess the intensity of malaria transmission in a specific area. It is calculated by the formula: API = (Number of positive blood slides for malaria / Total population) &times; 1,000 Given: Total population = 100,000 Number of slides examined = 50,000 Number of slides positive for malaria = 100 API= (confirmed cases during one year/population under surveillance) x 1000= (100/100000) x 1000= 1 Reference - Park's Preventive and social medicine, 27th EDITION Pg 306

Sample MCQ #3

According to WHO, what proportion of a country's Gross National Product (GNP) should ideally be allocated to health services?

A2%
B2.5%
C3.5%
D5%
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. 5%

Explanation: The WHO recommends that 5% of a country's GNP be spent on health services. This allocation is intended to: Ensure adequate healthcare delivery to the population Strengthen public health infrastructure Meet the healthcare needs of the community effectively

Sample MCQ #4 · Image-based

Which disease is targeted by the National Vector Borne Disease Control Program, where the insect shown below serves as the vector?

AChikungunya
BVisceral leishmaniasis
CMalayan filariasis
DJapanese encephalitis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Japanese encephalitis

Explanation:

The insect depicted in the image is the Culex mosquito, which serves as the vector for Japanese encephalitis.

Japanese encephalitis is a viral infection that affects the brain and is transmitted mainly by Culex mosquitoes.

This disease is included in the National Vector Borne Disease Control Program in areas where it is endemic.
