Sample Mycobacteria Tuberculosis MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

Primary pulmonary TB is defined as the initial exogenous infection with tubercle bacilli. What is the name of the complex when the primary complex gets calcified?

AGhon complex
BSimon focus
CRanke complex
DAssman focus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Ranke complex

Ranke Complex is formed when the primary complex gets calcified. Ghon focus refers to the initial nodular cavitations. When these involve hilar lymph nodes, it is called primary complex. Simon focus is the hematogenous seeding of apex of the lung in post primary TB. reactivated Simon focus with central caseation is called Assman's focus. Reference: Essentials of Medical Microbiology, by Apurba Sastry and Sandhya Bhat, 3rd edition, page 625

Sample MCQ #2

A patient presented to the urology department with urinary frequency, dysuria, nocturia and abdominal pain. The patient is afebrile at the moment but complains of low grade fever at night. Upon urinalysis,pus cells were found but no bacterial agent was isolated. Imaging revealed a thimble bladder. Which specimen should be collected next for establishing a diagnosis?

AEarly morning urine specimen
BBladder biopsy
CCE-CT-(URETHRAL SWAB)
DHIV DNA PCR(CYSTOSCOPY SAMPLE)
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Early morning urine specimen

The given clinical features, along with sterile pyuria in urinalysis raises a suspicion of genitourinary TB. It is seen when tubercular bacilli are disseminated hematogenously. It is diagnosed by collecting 3 early morning urine samples and processing them via culture in MGIT or LJ media or Genexpert Radiology may show strictures or perinephric abscesses. It is treated well with ATT Reference: Essentials of Medical Microbiology, by Apurba Sastry and Sandhya Bhat, 3rd edition, page 752

Sample MCQ #3

Isoniazid is one of the first line drugs for the treatment of tuberculosis. Which of the following layers of the cell wall does it inhibit, that also gives Mycobacteria its acid fastness?

ALipoarabinomannan
BPeptidoglycan
CLipopolysaccharide
DMycolic acid
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Mycolic acid

Mycolic acid layer is the principal constituent of the mycobacterial cell wall, made up of long chain fatty acids attached to arabinogalactan. It confers very low permeability to the cell wall and is responsible for acid fastness. Lipoarabinomannan facilitates the survival of tubercle bacilli within the macrophages. It is also used as a target antigen for the TB diagnosis. Peptidoglycan maintains the shape and rigidity of the cell. Lipopolysaccharide is found in gram negative bacteria. Reference: Essentials of Medical Microbiology, by Apurba Sastry and Sandhya Bhat, 3rd edition, page 624

Sample MCQ #6

Smear microscopy for the detection of Mycobacteria species is done by the Ziehl Neelsen staining method. One of its modifications is the Kinyoun's acid fast staining. Which of the following is not an advantage of this method over the ZN method?

AHeating is not required
BPhenol concentration in carbol fuschin is decreased
CDuration of carbol fuschin staining is more
DFluorescent dyes can be used
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Phenol concentration in carbol fuschin is decreased

The concentration of phenol in carbol fuschin is increased in the Kinyoun's method. It is also called the cold method as heating is not required. This method doesn't use fluorescent dyes. However, fluorescent staining with auramine rhodamine stains can also be done wherein the bacilli appear as brilliant yellow against a dark background. Reference : Essentials of Medical Microbiology, by Apurba Sastry and Sandhya Bhat, 3rd edition, page 628

Sample MCQ #7

Which strain is used for vaccination against Tuberculosis in India?

AEdmonsten Zagreb strain
BOka strain
CDanish 1331
DSA 14-14-4
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Danish 1331

In India, WHO recommended Danish 1331 strain of BCG is used. BCG is available in lyophilized form, and should be reconstituted before administration. This is done by using normal saline as diluent. Distilled water is never used as it is irritant. Once reconstituted; it has to be administered within 1 hour Administration of BCG:0.1 mL (0.1 mg TU) of BCG vaccine is administered above the insertion of left deltoid by intradermal route, using a 26 gauge tuberculin syringe. BCG is directly given to the newborn soon after birth. If not given at birth it can be given later, maximum up to 2 years. Edmonston Zagreb strain is used in measles vaccine Oka strain is used in chicken pox vaccine SA 14-14-4 is used in JE vaccine Reference: Essentials of Medical Microbiology, by Apurba Sastry and Sandhya Bhat, 3rd edition, page 635

Sample MCQ #8

A 25-year-old male presents with fever, cough, and night sweats. Further examination reveals increased respiratory rate, oral thrush and decreased breath sounds in the right middle lung field. Laboratory investigation report states a CD4 count of 60/L. The most likely underlying pathology is:

AAutoimmune disease with pneumonia.
BHIV with mycobacterium tuberculosis infection
CHypersensitivity pneumonitis.
DMycobacterium tuberculosis infection.
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. HIV with mycobacterium tuberculosis infection

Normal CD4 count is 400/&micro;L. Extremely low count of CD4+ T cells associated with Oral thrush is characteristic of HIV/AIDS. None of the other choices would be associated with this finding. Respiratory difficulties due to infection are frequently seen in HIV/AIDS patients. HIV weakens the immune system, increasing the risk of TB in people with HIV. HIV-TB coinfection is very common. Reference: Ananthanarayan and Paniker's Textbook of Microbiology, 10th Edition, Chapter 19, Page Number 187-190.
