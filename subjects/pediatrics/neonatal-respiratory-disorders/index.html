✓ Correct Answer: C. Dipalmitoyl phosphatidyl choline

AGlycerol + 2 fatty acids + po4 + ethanolamine

DGlycerol + 2 fatty acids + po4 + inositol

✓ Correct Answer: B. Glycerol + 2 fatty acids + po4 + choline

Sample MCQ #8

Which among the following is the most common cause of abdominal mass in a neonate?

AMulticystic Dysplastic Kidney
BWilms Tumour
COmphalocele
DMedullary Sponge Kidney
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Multicystic Dysplastic Kidney

Multicystic dysplastic kidney- MCDK is the most common cause of an abdominal mass in the newborn, but the vast majority are nonpalpable at birth. In most cases, it is discovered incidentally during prenatal sonography. Contralateral hydronephrosis is present in 5-10% of patients. Sonography shows the characteristic appearance of a kidney replaced by multiple cysts of varying sizes that do not communicate, and no identifiable parenchyma is present. Serial USGs done at 3 month initially and then every 6 monthly post infancy show regression of the cysts. Most cysts resolve by 7 years of age. References: Nelson 21 st Edition, Chapter 552; OP Ghai 10 th Edition, Pages 528-529.
