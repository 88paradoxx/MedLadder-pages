About Polio and AIDS MCQs

Sample Polio and AIDS MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

A child with HIV presents with features of pneumonia. Which of the following organisms would be most commonly responsible?

AMycobacterium tuberculosis
BCorynebacterium diphtheria
CPneumocystis jirovecii
DStreptococcus pyogenes
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Pneumocystis jirovecii

P.jirovecii (previously P. carinii) pneumonia is the opportunistic infection that led to the initial description of AIDS. This is one of the commonest AIDS defining illnesses in children; most cases occur between 3rd and 6th months of life. Reference: Ghai Essential Pediatrics, 9 th ed., pg. 225
