Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following is an example of an allosteric enzyme?

AHexokinase
BPhosphofructokinase
CChymotrypsin
DTrypsin
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Phosphofructokinase

Explanation: Phosphofructokinase-1 (PFK-1) is a classical allosteric enzyme and a key regulatory step of glycolysis, whose activity is modulated by allosteric effectors like ATP and citrate (inhibitors) and AMP, ADP, and fructose-2,6-bisphosphate (activators). These regulators bind to sites other than the active site (allosteric sites), causing conformational changes and leading to a sigmoidal reaction curve , unlike Michelis-Menten enzymes. Hexokinase shows feedback inhibition by glucose-6-phosphate but is not a classical allosteric enzyme. Chymotrypsin and trypsin are digestive proteases regulated by zymogen activation (proteolytic cleavage) and not by allosteric modulation.

Sample MCQ #5

In non competitive inhibition of enzymes

AKm increases whereas Vmax decreases
BKm increases whereas Vmax remain unchanged
CVmax increases while Km decreases
DVmax decreases while Km remains unchanged
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Vmax decreases while Km remains unchanged

Noncompetitive Inhibition A type of inhibition in which the inhibitor bind to a site distinct from the substrate binding site. Two different types are: Reversible Noncompetitive Inhibition (Only few Noncompetitive inhibition are reversible) Irreversible Noncompetitive Inhibition (Most of Noncompetitive are irreversible). Features of noncompetitive inhibition Inhibitor have no structural resemblance to substrate Mostly Irreversible (Except a few reversible non- competitive inhibition) Excess substrate do not abolish the inhibition Km remains the same Vmax decreases. Reference: Harper's 32nd edition, page 82,83.
