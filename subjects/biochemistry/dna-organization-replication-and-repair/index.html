Sample MCQ #4

Which of the following is true regarding reverse transcriptase enzymes?

ARNA polymerase having reverse transcriptase activity
BDNA dependent RNA polymerase
CRNA dependent DNA polymerase
DPresent in DNA virus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. RNA dependent DNA polymerase

Option A: RNA polymerase does not have reverse transcriptase activity, it has only transcriptase activity. Telomerase enzymes have reverse transcriptase activity. Option B: Reverse transcriptase enzyme is RNA dependent DNA polymerase, not DNA dependent RNA polymerase. Option C: Reverse transcriptase enzyme is RNA dependent DNA polymerase Option D: Present in RNA virus. There exists in one species of animal viruses (retroviruses) a class of enzymes capable of synthesizing a single-stranded and then a dsDNA molecule from a single-stranded RNA template. This polymerase, termed RNA-dependent DNA polymerase, or "reverse transcriptase," first synthesizes a DNA - RNA hybrid molecule utilizing the RNA genome as a template. Reference: Harper's illustrated biochemistry 31 st edition CH 35 PN 367

Sample MCQ #5

Which mineral is essential for the function of the enzyme DNA polymerase?

AIron
BZinc
CCopper
DMagnesium
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Magnesium

Explanation: DNA Polymerase is an essential enzyme responsible for DNA replication and repair. It reads the DNA template strand and synthesises a complementary strand by adding nucleotides in a 5' to 3' direction, ensuring the fidelity of DNA replication. Magnesium ions (Mg&#178;+) act as crucial cofactors for DNA polymerase. Magnesium stabilises the structure of nucleotides and the active site of the enzyme, facilitating the formation of phosphodiester bonds between nucleotides. This ion is essential for both the catalytic activity and the accuracy of DNA polymerase during DNA synthesis.

Sample MCQ #6

What is the role of the 3' to 5' exonuclease activity of DNA polymerase?

ARNA synthesis
BDNA unwinding
CPrimer removal
DProofreading
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Proofreading

Explanation: The 3&prime; to 5&prime; exonuclease activity of DNA polymerase functions in proofreading by removing incorrectly paired nucleotides from the 3&prime; end of the growing DNA strand and replacing them with the correct ones, thereby maintaining high fidelity of DNA replication. DNA unwinding is done by helicase, RNA synthesis by RNA polymerase, and primer removal occurs through 5&prime; to 3&prime; exonuclease activity (in prokaryotes) or RNase H and DNA polymerase &delta; (in eukaryotes).

Sample MCQ #8

Okazaki fragments is found on:

ARetrograde strand of DNA
BForward strand of DNA
CMessenger RNA
DRibosomal RNA
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Retrograde strand of DNA

DNA polymerases only synthesize DNA in the 5' to 3' direction. Because the DNA strands are antiparallel, the polymerase functions asymmetrically. On the leading (forward) strand, the DNA is synthesized continuously. On the lagging (retrograde) strand, the DNA is synthesized in short (1-5 kb) fragments, the so-called Okazaki fragments, so named after the scientist who discovered them. Several Okazaki fragments (up to 1000) must be sequentially synthesized for each replication fork. Option A: On the lagging (retrograde) strand, the DNA is synthesized in short fragments, called Okazaki fragments. Option B: On the leading (forward) strand, the DNA is synthesized continuously. Option C & D: Any RNA does not contain Okazaki fragments. Reference: Harper's illustrated biochemistry 32 nd edition CH 35 PN 363
