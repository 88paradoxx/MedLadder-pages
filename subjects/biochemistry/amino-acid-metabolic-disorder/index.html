Sample MCQ #3

RBC uses which of the following substances during fasting/Starvation?

AGlucose
BKetones
CAmino acids
DFatty acids
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Glucose

Glucose Is Always Required by the Central Nervous System and Erythrocytes Erythrocytes lack mitochondria and hence are wholly reliant on (anaerobic) glycolysis and the pentose phosphate pathway at all times. The brain can metabolize ketone bodies to meet about 20% of its energy requirements; the remainder must be supplied by glucose. The metabolic changes that occur in the fasting state and starvation serve to preserve glucose and the body&rsquo;s limited glycogen reserves for use by the brain and red blood cells, and to provide alternative metabolic fuels for other tissues. So Option A is the correct option.

✓ Correct Answer: B. Phenylacetate

Clinical Manifestations of phenylketonuria (PKU) - The affected infant is normal at Profound mental retardation develops gradually if the infant remains untreated. Cognitive delay may not be evident for the 1st few months. Some may have a seborrheic or eczematoid rash , which is usually mild and disappears as the child grows older. Sample MCQ #6

Which of the following is false regarding Phenylketonuria?

ADietary phenylalanine restriction is used as a treatment
BOccurs due to deficiency of phenylalanine hydroxylase
CDiet should contain high phenylalanine containing food items
DTyrosine should be supplied in the diet
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Diet should contain high phenylalanine containing food items

Phenylketonuria is due to deficiency of phenylalanine hydroxylase . Dietary restriction of phenylalanine with supplementation of tyrosine is needed as tyrosine is a non-essential amino acid synthesized from phenylalanine by the action of phenylalanine hydroxylase. Reference: Harper's 32nd edition, page 304

Sample MCQ #8

In Phenylketonuria, the main aim of first line therapy is:

AReplacement of the defective enzyme
BReplacement of the deficient product
CLimiting the substrate for deficient enzyme
DGiving the missing amino acid by diet
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Limiting the substrate for deficient enzyme

The primary goal of therapy is to reduce phenylalanine levels in the plasma and brain. Phenylketonuria - Hyperphenylalaninemia arise from defects in - Phenylalanine hydroxylase , (type I, classic phenylketonuria (PKU), frequency 1 in 10,000 births) Dihydrobiopterin reductase (type II and III) Dihydrobiopterin biosynthesis (type IV and V) Alternative catabolites are excreted. A diet low in phenylalanine can prevent the mental retardation of PKU. DNA probes facilitate prenatal diagnosis of defects in phenylalanine hydroxylase or dihydrobiopterin reductase. Treatment of Classical PKU A low-phenylalanine diet. Administration of large neutral amino acids is another approach to diet therapy.
