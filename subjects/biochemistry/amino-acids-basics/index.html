Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following biological important glycosamino glycans present in Cornea?

AHeparan Sulphate
BKeratan Sulphate II
CHyaluronic Acid
DKeratan Sulphate I
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Keratan Sulphate I

keratan sulfates consist of repeating Gal-GlcNAc disaccharide units containing sulfate attached to the 6 position of GlcNAc or occasionally of Gal. Keratan sulfate I was originally isolated from the cornea, while keratan sulfate II came from cartilage. In the eye, they lie between collagen fibrils and play a critical role in corneal transparency. Changes in proteoglycan composition found in corneal scars disappear when the cornea heals. Reference: Harper's 32 nd edition, Page no. 637

Sample MCQ #2

Amino sugar are formed forms ?

AGlucose- 1 -phosphate
BGlucose-6-phosphate
CFructose- 1 -phosphate
DFructose-6-phosphate
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Fructose-6-phosphate

Ans. is 'd' i.e., Fructose-6-phosphate [Ref Dinesh puri 3rdle p. 170] Amino sugars are dervatives of monosaccharides in which an amino group replaces the -OH residue on carbon-2 of hexose, such as glucose, galactose and mannose, The corresponding compounds are glucosamine, galactosamine and mannosamine, respectively. The amino acid group is usually acetylated, e.g. N- acetylglucosamine or N-actylgalactosamine. The amino sugars are required for the synthesis of glycolipids, glycoproteins and proteoglycans. They are synthesized from fructose-6-phosphate

✓ Correct Answer: A. Acidic Amino Acid

Sample MCQ #5

Which of the following is not an aromatic amino acid?

APhenylalanine
BTyrosine
CTryptophan
DValine
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Valine

Valine is not an aromatic amino acid. Aromatic amino acids: Histidine (with imidazole ring) Phenylalanine (benzene ring) Tyrosine (phenol ring) Tryptophan (indole ring) Reference: Harper's Illustrated Biochemistry, 32nd Edition, Page Number-205.

Sample MCQ #6

Which amino acid can protonate and deprotonate at neutral pH?

AHistidine
BLeucine
CGlycine
DArginine
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Histidine

Amino acid which can protonate and deprotonate means those which can act as a buffer . Amino acid whose pKa = pH of the medium has maximum buffering capacity . pKa of imidazole group of histidine is 6.5-7.4. At pH = 7, Imidazole group of histidine can act as buffer. Reference: Harper's 32nd edition, page 11,12
