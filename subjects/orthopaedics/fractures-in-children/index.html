Sample MCQ #1

What is the most common complication after successful treatment of a distal femoral physeal fracture?

ANonunion
BCompartment syndrome
CKnee stiffness
DPremature physeal closure
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Premature physeal closure

Following are some facts about fractures in children Fractures in children heal faster. Fractures close to the joint heal faster. Sideways displacement will remodel. Angulation in the plane of the adjacent joint is acceptable. Rotational malalignment will never remodel. Physis subjected to compressive forces inhibits growth Reference- Maheshwari 5th /ep. PG NO 59

Sample MCQ #2

Radial head excision is avoided in all except

AEssex Lopresti fracture
BDisplaced fracture in children
CUndisplaced fractures in adults
DComminuted fractures in adults
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Comminuted fractures in adults

The treatment of radial head fractures depend on the amount of comminution and the age of the patient. Comminuted fractures in adults is treated by excising the broken fragments. However the radial head excision is contraindicated in Essex Lopresti fracture - Combination of radial head fracture with interosseous membrane rupture and DRUJ subluxation. Displaced fracture in children - Excision of radial head in children will cause cubitus valgus Undisplaced fractures in adults - These can be treated with POP cast. Reference: J. Maheshwari's 5th Edition of Essential Orthopaedics page no. 107

Sample MCQ #6

Poor prognostic factors in Pott's paraplegia?

AYounger age
BGradual progression of paraplegia
CMotor paralysis alone
DLong standing paraplegia
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Long standing paraplegia

Prognosis of Pott's paraplegia depends upon the following factors: Age: Children respond to treatment better than adults. Onset: Gradual onset paraplegia has a better prognosis. Duration: Long standing paraplegia has a worse prognosis. Severity: Motor paralysis alone has a good prognosis. Sphincter involvement i.e., urinary or bowel incontinence are bad prognostic indicators. Progress: Sudden progress of the paraplegia has a bad prognosis. Poor prognostic factors in Pott's paraplegia are acute onset, sudden progression and long-standing paraplegia Reference: Maheshwari Essential Orthopedics, 7 th edition, Page no. 192.

Sample MCQ #7

Anorexic female presented with bleeding gums, loose teeth and subperiosteal hemorrhages. What is the defect?

AHydroxylation of lysine and proline
BVitamin K metabolism
CDeficiency of factor VIII
DThrombocytopenia
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Hydroxylation of lysine and proline

The clinical features are consistent with scurvy. Scurvy occurs due to deficiency of vitamin C Vitamin C is a coenzyme in the hydroxylation of proline and lysine. Hydroxyproline and hydroxylysine are the precursors of collagen synthesis. In scurvy, due to vitamin C deficiency, there is reduction in collagen synthesis. So, wherever collagen is required to provide structural integrity, problems occur. E.g. there is weakening of the capillary basement membrane leading to bleeding at sites through out the body. Reference: Miler's 8th Edition of Review of Orthopaedics, page no. 91

Sample MCQ #8

Which is the biochemical finding seen in renal osteodystrophy?

AElevated calcium
BElevated phosphate
CDecreased ALP
DDecreased PTH
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Elevated phosphate

The most important biochemical change in renal osteodystrophy is elevated phosphate levels. This is due to the inability of the failing kidney to excrete phosphate, leading to phosphate retention.
