Sample MCQ #1

Why fracture shaft femur is early stabilised

ATo prevent blood loss
BARDS
CNon union
DCompartment syndrome
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. To prevent blood loss

Answer- A. To prevent blood loss Fracture shaft femur can cause upto 2L of blood loss and severe hypotension, if not immoblized early. the fracture shaft femur should be immoblized early. This can be temporary immobilization by TT splint followed by surgery or can be by immediate surgery.

Sample MCQ #2

The MOST common type of dislocation of elbow joint is:

APosterior
BPosterolateral
CPosteromedial
DLateral
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Posterior

In adults, the elbow is the second most frequently dislocated major joint, after the shoulder. It is the most commonly dislocated joint in children. More than 90% of all elbow dislocations are posterior dislocations. This injury entails disengagement of the coronoid process of the ulna from the trochlea of the humerus with movement posteriorly. The mechanism of injury is typically a fall onto an outstretched hand with the elbow in extension upon impact.

Most common complication of inter trochanteric femur fracture is

AMalunion
BNon-union
CAvascular necrosis
DOsteoarthritis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Malunion

Most common complication of extracapsular inter trochanteric femur fracture is MALUNION. Most common complication of intracapsular neck of femur fracture is nonunion followed by AVN. Reference- Maheshwari ortho 5 th edition page no 139

Sample MCQ #6

What is not required in an emergency management of an open femur fracture?

AOpen Reduction Internal Fixation
BClosed Reduction External Fixation
CAntibiotic Coverage
DOpen Reduction External Fixation
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Open Reduction Internal Fixation

Open reduction and internal fixation is not done on emergency basis in cases of open fractures. First fracture should be stabilized with external fixator and wound should be debrided. Once wound gets healed and infection is cleared thereafter open reduction and internal fixation is done. For open fractures gustilo and Anderson classification is used for grading. For open fractures like 3B - antibiotics coated intramedullary nails are preferred over K wires. With the evolution of available nails like antibiotic impregnated nails, intramedullary nailing is now performed even in 3B fractures. Although there was initial phobia for the use of intramedullary nails, their usage has become widespread not only in grade 1 and 2 but even in grade 3 injuries. Reference:Anderson JT, Gustilo RB. Orthopedics clinics of North America, 1980; 11:56
