Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following drugs can be used in the treatment of GCT of pelvis?

AAlendronate
BZolindronic acid
CDenosumab
DTocilizumab
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Denosumab

Denosumab can be used in the treatment of GCT, especially in those tumors that arise in difficult to access areas such as the pelvis and the sacrum. Denosumab is a monoclonal antibody that blocks RANK ligands. The tumor cells in GCT acts via the RANK ligands to stimulate the osteoclasts. And its this activated osteoclasts that destroy the bone in GCT. Therefore, Denosumab can stop activation of osteoclasts, thereby causing shrinkage of the tumor. Reference: Miler's 8th Edition of Review of Orthopaedics, page no. 1895

Sample MCQ #2 · Image-based

A 50 year old male was involved in a RTA and sustained injury to his pelvis. X-ray taken is given below. Identify fracture?

AMalgaigne fracture
BStraddle fracture
CSacroiliac joint dislocation only
DDuverney's fracture
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Malgaigne fracture
  • The x ray shows fractures of right superior and inferior pubic rami with disruption of the ipsilateral sacroiliac joint. This is what is known as Malgaigne’s fracture.
  • Straddle fracture – bilateral superior and inferior pubic ramus fractures
  • Duverney’s fracture – isolated iliac wing fracture.

Reference- Maheshwari Essential Ortho 5th edition, page no 3
