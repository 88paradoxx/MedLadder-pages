Sample MCQ #2

Which of the following is an orthopedic emergency?

AFracture neck of femur
BSeptic arthritis
CLateral condyle humerus fracture
DGaleazzi fracture
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Septic arthritis

Septic arthritis is an absolute orthopedic emergency as delaying arthrotomy can cause serious joint damage. The pus in septic arthritis contains collagenases and metalloproteinases that start destroying articular cartilage within a period of 8 hours. So arthrotomy and lavage should be done as early as possible. Reference: Tachdjian's Pediatric Orthopaedics 5th edition page no. 1055

Sample MCQ #3

Non suppurative osteomyelites is called?

ABrodie's abscess
BGarre's osteomylitis
CChronic osteomyelitis
DSalmonella osteomyelitis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Garre's osteomylitis

GARRE'S OSTEOMYELITIS Sclerosing non suppurative chronic osteomyelitis Fusiform osseous enlargement. Cortical thickening No discharging sinus There is tenderness on deep palpation Shafts of the femur or tibia are most common site Treatment is guarded. Acute symptoms subside with rest and broad-spectrum antibiotics. Sometimes, making a gutter or holes in the bone bring relief in pain. Reference: Maheshwari Essential Orthopedics, 7 th edition, Page no. 173.

Sample MCQ #6

Cells involved in bone remodelling are

AOsteoclasts
BOsteoblasts
CBoth A and B
DOsteocytes
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Both A and B

Remodeling is the process by which bone gets reshaped according to external forces. This a slow, continuous and dynamic process. Here, some bone is removed form certain areas and some bone gets deposited in other areas, so both osteoblasts and osteoclasts are needed for remodeling. Reference: J. Maheshwari's 5th Edition of Essential Orthopaedics page no. 11 Reference: Campbell's Operative Orthopaedics 14th edition page no. 1186

Sample MCQ #7

Which bone cell produces osteonectin and osteocalcin?

AOsteoclast
BOsteocyte
COsteoblast
DOsteoprogenitor cell
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Osteoblast

Osteoblast is the bone building cell. It produces all the proteins needed to make up bone tissue, which includes type I collagen, osteocalcin, osteonectin etc It also produces an important enzyme, Alkaline phosphatase, which can be used as a marker for bone formation. Reference: Miler's 8th Edition of Review of Orthopaedics, page no. 18.

Sample MCQ #8

A 12 year old boy presented with a lytic lesion in proximal humerus. Which is the least likely cause?

APlasmacytoma
BMetastasis
CChondroblasoma
DSimple bone cyst
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Plasmacytoma

When a child comes with a lytic lesion in the proximal humerus there are 3 possibilities Simple bone cyst - It will appear as a cyst in the metaphysis. Chondroblastoma - It will appear as a lytic lesion in the epiphysis. Metastasis - Even though rare, mets can occur in the proximal humerus in a child e.g. neuroblastoma Plasmacytoma is a solitary myeloma and is always seen in elderly patients and never in this age group Reference: J. Maheshwari's 5th Edition of Essential Orthopaedics page no. 245
