Sample MCQ #1

What acts as both poison & antidote ?

ACuSO4
BHgC12
CAr203
DThallium arsenate
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. CuSO4

Ans. is 'a' i.e., CuSO4 [Ref: Reddy 30th/e p. 500, 503] Copper sulphate acts as a poison & an antidote for phosphorus poisoning. Copper sulphate when ingested causes burning pain in stomach with colicky abdominal pain, vomiting. In severe cases, hemolysis, hemoglobinuria, methemoglobinemia, jaundice, pancreatitis & cramps & convulsions. Death can occur due to hepatic & renal failure. It is given in gastric lavage for phosphorous poisoning as it coats the particles of phosphorous with a film of copper sulphide which is harmless.

Sample MCQ #2

If father's blood group is A and mother's blood group is B, the child's blood group will be

AA only
BB only
CAB only
DAB, A, B or O
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. AB, A, B or O

There are 4 phenotypes and 6 genotypes in blood group inheritance. If father is phenotype A (Genotype may be AA or AO) and mother is phenotype B (Genotype may be BB or BO), the offspring's genotype can be AB or AO or BO or OO, which will manifest the genotype as AB or A or B or O. A and B are both "dominant" and O is "recessive" to A and B, whereas A and B are equally dominant. Reference: The Essentials of Forensic Medicine & Toxicology, KS Narayan Reddy, 35th edition, Page No. 351

Sample MCQ #4

Which of the following statements about blood grouping is not correct?

AIt can be sued to resolve confusion of identity in alleged exchange of babies in maternity unit.
BIt is the method to conclusively fix the paternity.
CIt can assist in matching fragmented human remains in mass disasters.
DIt can help to show whether the blood stain on the weapon belongs to the suspect or victim.
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. It is the method to conclusively fix the paternity.

Blood grouping helps to solve paternity problems but does not conclusively fix paternity. DNA Profiling (Fingerprinting) provides absolute certainty and is a fool proof method to conclusively fix paternity. Comparing the blood grouping helps to certain extent in case of exchange of babies in maternity hospitals, to match the fragmentary remains of skeleton and to compare the blood stains on the weapons. Reference: The Essentials of Forensic Medicine & Toxicology, KS Narayan Reddy, 35th edition, Page No. 354

Sample MCQ #6

Which of the following statements about blood grouping is not correct?

AIt can be used to resolve confusion of identity in alleged exchange of babies in maternity unit.
BIt is the method to conclusively fix the paternity.
CIt can assist in matching fragmented human remains in mass disasters.
DIt can help to show whether the blood stain on the weapon belongs to the suspect or victim.
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. It is the method to conclusively fix the paternity.

Explanation: Blood grouping does not conclusively fix the paternity. Blood grouping can only exclude, not confirm, paternity, because many individuals share the same blood type, so a match merely indicates possibility rather than proof, while an incompatible type definitively rules someone out. DNA Profiling (Fingerprinting) provides absolute certainty and is a fool proof method to conclusively fix paternity. Comparing the blood grouping helps to certain extent in case of exchange of babies in maternity hospitals, to certain extent to match the fragmentary remains of skeleton and to certain extent to compare the blood stains on the weapons (Option A, B, D)

Sample MCQ #7

Gastric lavage is contraindicated in which of the following: September 2010

AArsenic poisoning
BKerosene poisoning
COrganophosphorus Poisoning
DDhatura poisoning
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Kerosene poisoning

Ans. B: Kerosene Poisoning Lavage is contraindicated when patients have a compromised, unprotected airway and in patients at risk of gastrointestinal hemorrhage or perforation. Relative contraindications include when the poisoning is due to a corrosive substance, hydrocarbons, or for poisons that have an effective antidote.

Sample MCQ #9 · Image-based

Identify the technique of substance abuse demonstrated in the given image.

ASnorting
BBagging
CSniffing
DHuffing
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Bagging

Explanation:

Bagging involves placing volatile substances in a plastic bag and inhaling the vapors directly by covering the nose and mouth. The method allows concentrated inhalation of substances like glue, paint, or solvents, leading to euphoria, hallucinations, and central nervous system depression.

Explanation of other options:

Snorting (Option A): Snorting involves inhaling powdered drugs through the nostrils after they are finely crushed. A straw, rolled paper, or similar device is commonly used to deliver the powder into the nasal passages.

Sniffing (Option C): Sniffing refers to simply inhaling the vapors or odors of volatile or aromatic substances directly through the nose, often from open containers or surfaces.

Huffing (Option D): Huffing is the intentional inhalation of vapors from volatile substances or chemicals directly from their container, soaked cloth, or other sources. It is a form of recreational inhalant abuse associated with significant health risks.

Key takeaways:

  • Bagging is a dangerous form of solvent abuse involving suffocation risk.
  • Commonly abused substances include toluene and glue.
  • Chronic use can cause hypoxia, arrhythmias, and brain damage.
