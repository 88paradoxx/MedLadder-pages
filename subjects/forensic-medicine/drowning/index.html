Sample Drowning MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

On examining a case of wet drowning, you observe weights that are attached to the body to keep it underwater. What does this suggest?

AHomicidal drowning
BSuicidal drowning
CCan be either homicidal or suicidal
DAccidental drowning
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Can be either homicidal or suicidal

Attachment of weights to the body to keep it underwater is consistent with both homicidal and suicidal drowning. Differentiating points: Suicidal drowning - Determined suicidal person may attach weights to the body or tie his limbs. It is common among women. Homicidal drowning &ndash; The assailant may attach weight to his victim&rsquo;s body to keep it underwater to conceal the evidence. In addition, there may be signs of struggle on the body, or evidence of struggle at the banks of the river from where the body was recovered. Accidental drowning is the most common form of drowning. It is seen among fishermen, bathers, dock workers, intoxicated, and epileptics. Homicidal drowning is the least common. Reference: Knight's Forensic Pathology, 4th edition, Page No. 400; Textbook of Forensic Medicine & Toxicology, Anil Aggrawal, Page No. 402

Sample MCQ #2

Incorrect about Paltauf's haemorrhage:

ASign of drowning
BSubpleural haemorrhage
CMostly seen in middle lobe
DAll are true
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Mostly seen in middle lobe

Paltauf's haemorrhages are mostly found in the lower lobes . Paltauf&rsquo;s haemorrhages ( Paltauf&rsquo;s spots): Appearance &rarr; Shining, pale pink or bluish red haemorrhages, immediately beneath the pleurae (Subpleural space). Size &rarr; Minute or up to 3-5 cm in diameter Incidence &rarr; 50%. Location &rarr; Lower lobes of lungs (most common). May also be on interlobar surfaces or anterior surfaces of entire lungs. Mechanism &rarr; Tearing (rupture) of the interalveolar partitions due to decreased pressure during forced expirations They are found when the alveolar walls rupture as a result of increased pressure during forced expirations. They are described in 50- 60% of drownings. They are mostly found in the lower lobes on the anterior surface and margins of lung. Reference : Textbook of Forensic Medicine & Toxicology, Anil Aggrawal, Page No. 397

Sample MCQ #3

Characteristic feature of drowning is Specific finding of antemortem drowning:

ACadaveric spasm with grass in hand
BWasherwoman's hand
CCutis anserina Water in stomach
DDiatoms
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Cadaveric spasm with grass in hand

Cadaveric spasm with grass in hand is the definite sign of antemortem drowning. (The medico-legal importance of diatoms is controversial) Definite signs of antemortem drowning: Cadaveric Spasm with weeds/grass in the hand Emphysema aquosum Copious amount of white, fine, leathery froth (D/t Emphysema Acquosum) Paltauf&rsquo;s haemorrhages Diatoms ( The medico-legal importance of diatoms is controversial. If the same species of diatoms present in the drowning media is present in bone marrow and if the victim had not drunk the same water before submersion is the most important internal sign of antemortem drowning) References: The Essentials of Forensic Medicine & Toxicology, KS Narayan Reddy, 35th edition, Page No. 286, Textbook of Forensic Medicine & Toxicology, Anil Aggrawal, Page No. 395
