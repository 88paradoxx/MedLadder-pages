Sample Musculoskeletal Imaging MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following is used in bone scan?

ATc-99m MDP
BTc-99m IOD
CTc-99m Na
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Tc-99m MDP

The radiotracer most commonly used for bone scan is Tc-99m methylene diphosphonate (MDP), where technetium-99-m is the radioactive component and MDP the biological component. Reference: Mc-Graw Hill speciality Board review Radiology page number 721

Sample MCQ #2

Three phase bone scan is positive in?

AOsteosarcoma
BOsteomyelitis
COsteogenesis imperfecta
DFibrous dysplasia
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Osteomyelitis

Three phase bone scan is done in osteomyelitis. Characteristic finding in osteomyelitis in a &ldquo;three-phase bone scan&rdquo; is increased activity in the blood flow, blood pool phases and delayed phase. Reference: Mc-Graw Hill speciality Board review Radiology page number 726
