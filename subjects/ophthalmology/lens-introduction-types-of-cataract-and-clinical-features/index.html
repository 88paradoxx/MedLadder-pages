Sample MCQ #1

What is not a cause of monocular diplopia

ASubluxation of lens
BKeratoconus
CDouble pupil
DIncomitant squint
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Incomitant squint

Monocular Diplopia Monocular diplopia is usually attributable to refractive or media abnormalities. Common causes include uncorrected astigmatism, corneal irregularities, tear film abnormalities, cataract, etc. The hallmark of monocular diplopia from refractive abnormalities is improvement with pinhole. KIDS(pneumonic) Keratoconus/astigmatism Incipient cataract Double pupil/trauma Subluxated lens Reference: kanski, 5 th Edition, Page no. 544.

Sample MCQ #2

What is the inheritance pattern of Wagner's syndrome ?

AX-linked recessive
BAutosomal dominant
CAutosomal recessive
DMitochondrial
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Autosomal dominant

Wagner's syndrome has an autosomal dominant (AD) inheritance with following features: Vitreous is liquified with condensed membranes. Retina shows narrow and sheathed vessels, and pigmented spots in the periphery. Choroid may be atrophied. Cataract may develop as late complication. Reference : Comprehensive Ophthalmology 7 th ed. &ndash; A.K.Khurana, Ch-12 Diseases of retina

Sample MCQ #3

The most frequent cataract type seen in adult retinitis pigmentosa patient is

APSC
BAnterior polar
CCortical
DMixed
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. PSC

Cataracts are recognized as the most common anterior segment complication of RP. The opacity of lens generally happens at the mid stage of the disease course with obvious clinical symptoms and signs, and the major symptom is glare, especially in bright light. As these are complicated cataracts and are posterior subcapsular cataracts the most common prob is that of glare. Reference: parson's 22/e section IV, chapter 20. pg-327-328. RP

Sample MCQ #4

Crystalline lens absorbs light rays shorter than:

A295 nm
B350 nm
C390 nm
D490 nm
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. 390 nm

The crystalline lens absorbs almost all incident energy to wavelengths of nearly 400 nm. In youth, a very small amount of UV-A reaches the retina, but the lens becomes more absorbing with age. Reference: Khurana Optics 2/e

Sample MCQ #5

Accommodation is maximum at the age of:

A25 years
B5 years
C14 years
D30 years
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. 5 years

Accommodation is a dynamic process in which the curvature of the eye's natural lens is temporarily adjusted to improve focus at near or in eyes that are hyperopic (far-sighted). Accommodative convergence described the normal convergence of the eyes in response to accommodation of the lens It is strongest in children, gets weakened with age Reference: Khurana Optics 2/e

Sample MCQ #6

Which of the following is most common type of congenital cataract causing diminished vision?

ABlue dot Cataract
BZonular Cataract
CSutural Cataract
DCoronary Cataract
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Zonular Cataract

Zonular or lamellar cataract is the most common type of congenital cataract associated with diminished vision. A zone in the foetal nucleus is usually affected. Hypocalcemia in pregnancy is an important cause. Reference: Yanoff and Duker 4th edition 415/416

Sample MCQ #7

Rubella in pregnancy is important cause in causing which of the following cataracts?

ABlue dot Cataract
BZonular Cataract
CNuclear Cataract
DCoronary Cataract
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Nuclear Cataract

Nuclear cataract usually affects the embryonic nucleus . Rubella in pregnancy is an important cause. In maternal rubella infection, after 6 weeks of gestation , the virus can penetrate the lens capsule causing unilateral or bilateral, dense, nuclear opacities at birth, or they may develop several weeks or months later. Extra information - lamellar/ zonular cataract can also be seen in rubella. The congenital rubella retinitis may present as a salt and-pepper fundus appearance. Reference: Yanoff and Duker 4th edition 415/416
