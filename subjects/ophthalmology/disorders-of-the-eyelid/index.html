Sample MCQ #1

Most common malignant tumour of eyelid is ?

ASebaceous gland carcinoma
BBasal cell carcinoma
CSquamous cell carcinoma
DMalignant melanoma
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Basal cell carcinoma

Ans, b. Basal cell carcinoma Basal cell carcinoma is the most common malignant tumour of the eyelids and constitutes 85-90% of all malignant epithelialeyelid tumours.

BBCC
CSCC
DMelanoma

✓ Correct Answer: B. BCC

The most common malignancy of the eyelid is basal cell carcinoma. Reference: Robin's basic pathology 10 th edition, 1308

Sample MCQ #3

Extra row of cilia in the lid margin is

ATrichiasis
BDistichiais
CEntropion
DMadarosis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Distichiais

Distichiasis is a rare condition in which there is an extra posterior row of cilia , occasionally in all four lids. The posterior row of cilia arise from the orifices of the Meibomian glands . Reference: Kanski 8/e, Pg. 32.

Sample MCQ #4

When planning to surgically repair a congenital ptosis in a patient, what is the most important pre-operative assessment?

AIntercanthal distance
BLevator function
CRecti function
DEyelid crease height
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Levator function

The surgical technique used for congenital ptosis depends primarily on levator function; patients with poor levator function often require some form of frontalis sling. The severity (in mm) of ptosis is secondary. Patients with marked ptosis and good levator function can still benefit from levator surgery. Reference: Kanski 8th edition/41-44

Sample MCQ #5

Chronic unilateral blepharoconjunctivitis is commonly a presenting sign of which one of the following?

ASquamous cell carcinoma
BBasal cell carcinoma
CCutaneous melanoma
DSebaceous carcinoma
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Sebaceous carcinoma

Sebaceous cell carcinoma commonly presents as a unilateral blepharoconjunctivitis. The other entities would be much less likely to involve the conjunctival surface of the eyelid to a significant degree and usually present differently. Reference: Kanski 8th edition/19

Sample MCQ #6

Ectropion and loss of eyelashes should alert one to the possibility of which one of the following?

AFacial nerve (VII) palsy
BChronic eyelid webbing
CInvolutional ectropion
DMalignancy
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Malignancy

Ectropion combined with alteration of eyelid anatomy, or loss of eyelashes, should suggest the possibility of malignancy and need for a biopsy. Loss of eyelashes is not typical of involutional changes, facial palsy, or eyelid rubbing. Reference: Kanski 8th edition 13/45

Sample MCQ #7

What is the most important predictor for recurrence and survival in patients with eyelid skin lesion?

AExcision margins
BTumor thickness
CDiameter
DGeographic Location on eyelid
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Tumor thickness

Breslow thickness is the most important predictor of recurrence and survival in patients with eyelid skin melanoma. Reference: Kanski 8th edition/19

Sample MCQ #8

All the following are surgeries for ptosis correction except

ABrow suspension
BLevator resection
CMuller resection
DMuller recession
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Muller recession

Muller recession -Weakening procedure Muller resection -Strengthening procedure In ptosis, Muller's resection needs to be done. Muller's muscle is an eyelid elevator which is weakened in ptosis. Reference: Kanski 8/e, Pg. 42 -44.
