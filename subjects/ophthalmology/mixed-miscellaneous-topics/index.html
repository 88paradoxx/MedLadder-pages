Sample MCQ #1

Chronic use of systemic steroids most commonly leads to which ocular complication?

ACataract
BGlaucoma
CUveitis
DConjunctival and lid papillomatosis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Cataract

Injudicious use of systemic steroids leads to the following ocular complications: Cataract - most common Delayed wound healing Infections of eye Papilloedema Central retinal vein occlusion Glaucoma - more often associated with topical ocular or periocular steroids Reference: Kanski, 7th Edition, Pg no. 461.

Sample MCQ #2

Which of the following is not used as local injection for ophthalmic use?

ATriamcinolone
BPrednisolone
CMethylprednisolone
DDexamethasone
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Methylprednisolone

All of the following are examples of steroids. Methylprednisolone is administered only systemically . The remaining steroids can be administered locally or systemically. Reference: Kanski, 7th Edition, Pg no. 459.
