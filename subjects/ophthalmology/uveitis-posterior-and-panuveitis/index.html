Sample MCQ #1

Uveal effusion syndrome may be associated with all of the following, except

AMyopia
BCiliochoroidal detachment
CStructural defect in sclera
DNanophthalmos
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Myopia

Uveal Effusion Syndrome(Ciliochoroidal Effusion) is an idiopathic disease of the retina and choroid characterized by abnormal accumulation of serous fluid in the outer layer of ciliary body or choroid. Clinical Features: Structurally abnormal sclera(Thick Sclera) Abnormal accumulation of serous fluid in the outer layer of ciliary body and choroid leading to Detachment of Ciliary body(Exudative) Detachment of Choroid(Exudative) Deep Ciliochoridal detachments Detachment of Retina(Exudative) Absence of inflammation or neoplastic disease Eyes maybe nanopthalmic (small) or hypermetropic Phthisis and hypotonia is usually due to ciliary body shutdown from chronic detachment which compromises the blood supply to the ciliary processes Mottling of pigment epithelium( Leopard spots ) may be seen after spontaneous resolution. Reference: Kanski, 7th Edition, Pg no. 484.

ASyphilis

Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following is true about Birdshot Chorioretinopathy?

AUnilateral
BBilateral
CAcute onset
DMost commonly seen in males
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Bilateral

Birdshot chorioretinopathy is an uncommon idiopathic chronic/recurrent bilateral disease which predominantly affects females . Over 95% patients are positive for HLA A29 . The disease involves both the retina and choroid independently. Reference: Kanski, 7th Edition, Pg no. 429, 467.

Sample MCQ #4

An individual infected with HIV has a necrotizing retinitis on fundus examination. Which of the following will be most helpful to make a diagnosis?

ASerologic testing for herpes virus, including CMV
BMantoux test
CVitreous biopsy
DBlood culture for herpes virus, including CMV
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Vitreous biopsy

Infection is the most likely cause of necrotizing retinitis in a HIV patient . The most common causes could be viral or parasitic(Toxoplasmosis). Vitreous biopsy is the most specific way to determine the cause. Option a/d - blood culture and serologic testing do not prove an intraocular infection, they are helpful only in ruling out the possibility of a viral infection. Option b - due to anergy, unreliable Reference: Kanski, 7th Edition, Pg no. 450.

Sample MCQ #5

A 20 year old man complains of difficulty in reading the newspaper with his right eye, three weeks after sustaining a gun shot injury to his left eye. The most likely diagnosis is

AMacular edema
BSympathetic ophthalmitis
COptic nerve avulsion
DDelayed vitreous hemorrhage
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Sympathetic ophthalmitis

Bilateral granulomatous non suppurative panuveitis after penetrating injury to ciliary body(Danger area of eye) It is thought that the penetrating trauma or surgery activates the afferent phase of an autoimmune reaction. This may be due to the exposure of sequestered ocular antigens to the immune system after trauma or an alteration of the immune tolerance to ocular antigens Occurs between 2 weeks to 3 months after injury, but cases are seen as early as 1 week and late as 10 years also. Symptom - loss of accommodation, photophobia Signs - Retrolental flare(inflammation behind lens) 1 st sign Hence loss of accommodation is the first symptom in this condition Reference: 9th/e kanski chapter 12 trauma pg. 448

Sample MCQ #6

Which of the following structures is not a part of the posterior segment of the eye?

AVitreous
BRetina
CChoroid
DCrystalline lens
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Crystalline lens

Explanation: Inside the eye, the globe is broadly divided into the anterior segment and posterior segment by the lens. The posterior segment, i.e. the part of the eye that lies behind the lens, is bounded internally by the ciliary body anteriorly and retina posteriorly. Structures present in the posterior segment include the retina, choroid, and the optic disc, and the cavity is filled with a transparent gel-like substance called the vitreous humour.

Sample MCQ #7

What organisms are responsible for acute retinal necrosis (ARN) and progressive outer retinal necrosis (PORN)?

ARubella and rubeola
BVaricella zoster and herpes simplex
CBorrelia bergdorferi and Bartonella henselae
DMycobacterium tuberculosis and Treponema pallidum
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Varicella zoster and herpes simplex

The organisms most commonly responsible for acute retinal necrosis (ARN) and progressive outer retinal necrosis (PORN) are varicella-zoster and herpes simplex. Rubella and rubeola do not cause acute retinitis in post-natal humans. Borrelia bergdorferi is the causitive organism of Lyme disease. Bartonella henselae is the causitive organism of cat-scratch disease. Mycobacterium tuberculosis and Treponema pallidum may cause retinitis and/or choroiditis, but are not the causitive organisms of acute retinal necrosis (ARN) and progressive outer retinal necrosis (PORN). Reference: Kanski 8th edition/439-440
