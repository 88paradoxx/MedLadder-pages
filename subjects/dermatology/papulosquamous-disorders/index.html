Sample MCQ #1

ATrichomonas vaginalis
BHuman immunodeficiency virus
CNeisseria gonorrhoeae
DHuman Papillomavirus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Human Papillomavirus

Genital HPV remains the most common sexually transmitted pathogen in the United States, infecting 60% of a cohort of initially HPV-negative, sexually active Washington state college women within 5 years in a study conducted from 1990 to 2000-i.e., during the pre-HPV immunization era. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21 st ed, Ch: 136, Pg: 1079

BHerpes simplex

DCandida
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. C.trachomatis

Most common cause of urethritis in males is N. gonorrhoea. The most common cause of Non-Gonococcal urethritis in males is Chlamydia trachomatis. The most common cause of urethritis in females is Chlamydia. Hence chlamydia is the most appropriate answer. Reference: Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine, 21 st Ed, Ch:136 Pg: 1081, 1083

Sample MCQ #3

Ichy purple papule followed by hayperpigmentation on resolution, is seen in?

AAddison's disease
BDM
CHypothyroidism
DLichen planus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Lichen planus

Ans. D. Lichen planus LP is characterized by shiny, violaceous, flat-topped polygonal papules which retain the skin lines. White lines, known as Wickham's striae, may traverse the surface of the papules. Linear lesions often appear along scratch marks or in scars (Koebner phenomenon). In most cases, the papules eventually flatten after a 6 months to 2 yrs, often to be replaced by an area of hyperpigmentation.

Sample MCQ #7

Not a histological feature of lichen planus is -

AAcanthosis
BHyperkeratosis
CNeutrophils in stratum corneum
DPigment incontinence
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Neutrophils in stratum corneum

Histopathology in Lichen planus The basic pathology in lichen planus is the damage to the basal cell layer of epidermis leading to degeneration of basal cells. Epidermal thickening especially of granular cell layer &rarr; Hypergranulosis. Hyperkeratosis (Thickening of stratum corneum) Acanthosis (Thickening of Stratum) malpighii. Subepidermal - lichenoid band due to deposition of lymphocytes & histiocytes in upper dermis. Dropping of melanin pigment from damaged keratinocytes of epidermis into dermis &rarr; pigment incontinence. This melanin is engulfed by macrophages which results information of cytoid bodies (civatte or colloid bodies). Max Joseph Histological cleft &rarr; Separation of epidermis in small clefts. Reference: Rooks textbook of dermatology, 9 th edition, chapter 37, page 14

Sample MCQ #8

'Claw clutching a Ball' appearance is seen in histopathology of which disease?

ALichen planus
BPityriasis rosea
CLichen scrofulosorum
DLichen nitidus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Lichen nitidus

The hallmark of lichen nitidus is a well-circumscribed granulomatous infiltrate of lymphocytes, macrophages, Langerhans giant cells, and multinucleated epithelioid histiocytes in the papillary dermis bordered by extensions of epidermal ridges. This sharply circumscribed inflammatory infiltrate generally spans 4 to 5 dermal papillae within the dermis, giving a "ball-in-clutch" configuration Reference: Rooks textbook of dermatology, 9 th edition, chapter 37, page 10

Sample MCQ #9 · Image-based

A patient presents with asymptomatic rash as shown below. What is the diagnosis ?

ATinea corporis
BPityriasis rosea
CSeborrheic dermatitis
DPityriasis alba
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Pityriasis rosea

Pityriasis rosea is an acute self-limiting papulosquamous disorder. It is characterized by a herald patch followed by scaly oval patches on the trunk and proximal extremities along the Langer's lines of cleavage giving characteristic "Christmas-tree" appearance.

Collarette scaling is common

Pityriasis rosea means rose-colored scale.

Reference: Rooks textbook of dermatology, 9th edition, chapter 25, page 92
