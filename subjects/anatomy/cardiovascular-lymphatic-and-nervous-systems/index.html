Sample Cardiovascular, Lymphatic and Nervous Systems MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

In about 85% of hearts, the posterior interventricular artery is a branch of which coronary artery?

ARight coronary artery
BLeft anterior descending artery
CLeft circumflex artery
DObtuse marginal artery
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Right coronary artery

Right coronary dominance is present in about 85% of hearts, where the posterior interventricular artery arises from the right coronary artery. In left dominance (about 8-10%), it arises from the circumflex artery.

Sample MCQ #2

The thoracic duct drains lymph into which venous confluence?

ARight venous angle
BLeft venous angle
CConfluence of the brachiocephalic veins
DAzygos vein
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Left venous angle

The thoracic duct, the largest lymphatic vessel, ascends from the cisterna chyli, crosses to the left at about T5, and drains at the left venous angle, the junction of the left internal jugular and left subclavian veins.

Sample MCQ #3

The middle meningeal artery enters the cranial cavity through which foramen?

AForamen ovale
BForamen spinosum
CForamen rotundum
DSuperior orbital fissure
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Foramen spinosum

The middle meningeal artery, a branch of the maxillary artery, enters the skull through the foramen spinosum. Its rupture, often from a fracture at the pterion, causes an extradural hematoma.

Sample MCQ #4

Approximately what proportion of breast lymph drains to the axillary lymph nodes?

A25%
B50%
C75%
D95%
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. 75%

About 75% of breast lymph drains to the axillary nodes, especially the anterior (pectoral) group. The remainder drains mainly to the internal mammary and supraclavicular nodes.

Sample MCQ #5

The cisterna chyli is typically located at the level of which vertebrae?

AT10-T11
BL1-L2
CL4-L5
DS1-S2
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. L1-L2

The cisterna chyli is a sac-like dilation at the origin of the thoracic duct, lying anterior to the L1-L2 vertebrae and to the right of the abdominal aorta. It receives lymph from the intestinal and lumbar trunks.

Sample MCQ #6

The sinoatrial nodal artery most commonly arises from which vessel?

ALeft circumflex artery
BRight coronary artery
CLeft anterior descending artery
DPosterior interventricular artery
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Right coronary artery

The sinoatrial node is supplied by the right coronary artery in about 60-65% of people and by the circumflex artery in the remainder. The atrioventricular node is supplied by the right coronary artery in about 80%.

Sample MCQ #7

An enlarged left supraclavicular lymph node (Virchow node) most classically suggests malignancy of which site?

ABreast
BStomach and abdominal organs
CLung apex
DThyroid
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Stomach and abdominal organs

The left supraclavicular node receives lymph via the thoracic duct from the abdomen, so enlargement (Troisier sign) classically indicates an abdominal or gastrointestinal malignancy, most famously gastric cancer.

Sample MCQ #8

Which vessel of the circle of Willis connects the internal carotid artery to the posterior cerebral artery?

AAnterior communicating artery
BPosterior communicating artery
CBasilar artery
DAnterior cerebral artery (A1 segment)
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Posterior communicating artery

The posterior communicating artery connects the internal carotid artery to the posterior cerebral artery on each side, completing the posterior part of the circle of Willis and linking the carotid and vertebrobasilar systems.
