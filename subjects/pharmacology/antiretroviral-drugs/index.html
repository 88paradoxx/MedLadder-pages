Sample MCQ #1

What is the first line regimen proposed by NACO for the treatment of HIV-2 infection?

A2NRTI+1NNRTI
B2NRTI+1Proteaseinhibitor
COnly NRTI
DAny of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. 2NRTI+1Proteaseinhibitor

NACO recommends a combination of 2NRTI+protease inhibitor for treatment of HIV-2 infection. Currently, the first line regimen consists of Tenofovir +Lamivudine +lopinavir -ritonavir Reference : Essentials of Medical Microbiology, by Apurba Sastry and Sandhya Bhat, 3rd edition, page 335

Sample MCQ #2

Lamivudine is a Nucleoside Reverse Transcriptase inhibitor (NRTI), which has proven antiviral activity against HIV and HBV. However, some patients infected with HBV are resistant to Lamivudine. Which mutation confers this property of drug resistance?

AYMDD gene
BPre-core mutation
CEscape mutation
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. YMDD gene

Hepatitis B virus infected patients on lamivudine therapy may develop resistance to the drug due to mutation in the YMDD locus present in the HBV reverse transcriptase region of polymerase gene. Pre-core mutants have mutations in pre-core region (which abolishes HBeAg production) or basal core promoter region (down regulates HBeAg production). The escape mutants of HBV have mutations in the S gene ("a" antigen region) which leads to alteration of HBsAg. Reference : Bailey and Love's Short Practice of Surgery, 28th Edition, Chapter: 20, Pg: 279

Sample MCQ #3

A resident doctor developed needle prick injury while taking blood samples of HIV positive symptomatic patients. Which of the following regimen is best for post exposure prophylaxis of HIV for this doctor?

AZidovudine + Lamivudine
BTenofovir + Lamivudine + Lopinavir
CTenofovir + Zidovudine + Emtricitabine
DZidovudine + Stavudine + Nevirapine
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Tenofovir + Lamivudine + Lopinavir

Post-exposure prophylaxis: Treatment should be started within 72 hours of exposure, preferably as early as possible At first the wound is washed with soap and water. Secondly before initiating drug therapy blood samples are taken to measure the baseline viral markers Then, triple drug therapy is given which includes Tenofovir, Lamivudine and a boosted protease inhibitor for 28 days. After 6 weeks again viral markers are measured to check for the infection.

Sample MCQ #4

Administration of which of the following drugs would increase the bioavailability of saquinavir?

ARitonavir
BVitamin C
CGanciclovir
DCimetidine
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Ritonavir

Ritonavir being a potent CYP3A4 inhibitor acts as a booster for other antiretrovirals like Saquinavir. Ritonavir (RTV) It is a potent PI; also a potent CYP3A4 inhibitor. Drug interactions, nausea, diarrhoea, paresthesias, fatigue and lipid abnormalities are prominent. Though RTV (600 mg twice daily) can be used as an antiretroviral drug, it is more commonly employed in a low dose (100 mg BD) to boost all other PIs except NFV. Low dose RTV produces no additional side effects. Sample MCQ #5

Which drugs can be used as pre exposure prophylaxis of HIV?

ATenofovir
BEmtricitabine
CEfavirenz
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. All of the above

Tenofovir is often co-administered with emtricitabine, and a once-daily, fixed-dose combination formulation is available, either alone or in combination with efavirenz. A recent placebo controlled. study found that use of emtricitabine and tenofovir was effective as pre-exposure prophylaxis, reducing HIV acquisition in men who have sex with men. In another placebo-controlled study, use of the experimental 1% tenofovir gel as a vaginal microbicide was effective in decreasing the incidence of heterosexual HIV acquisition. Reference : LANG&rsquo; Basic & clinical pharmacology by Bertram G. Katzung, Susen B. Masters and Anthony J. Trevor 12 th edition. Chapter 49- Antiviral Agents by Dr. Sharon Safrin,MD. Page no. 875

Sample MCQ #7

Which of the following is not the ART agent?

ANRTIs
BNNRTIs
CCCR4 receptor antagonist
DFusion inhibitors
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. CCR4 receptor antagonist

Six classes of antiretroviral agents are currently available for use: nucleoside/nucleotide reverse transcriptase inhibitors (NRTIs), nonnucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitors (NNRTIs), protease inhibitors (PIs), fusion inhibitors, CCR5 receptor antagonist integrase inhibitors.
