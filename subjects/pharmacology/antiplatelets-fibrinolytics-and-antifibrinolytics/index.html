Sample MCQ #1

Romiplostim acts on which of the following receptors:

AThrombopoeitin
BIL 6
CIL 8
DPGE 1
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Thrombopoeitin

Romiplostim: Genetically engineered protein in which the Fc component of a human antibody is fused to two copies of a peptide that stimulates the thrombopoietin receptors; approved for treatment of idiopathic thrombocytopenic purpura Ref: Katzung 11th edition Chapter 33.

Sample MCQ #2

All of the following are false regarding prasugrel, except:

ANot much metabolism in the body
BSlow onset of action than clopidogrel
CReserve inhibitors of ADP
DContraindicated in cerebrovascular accident
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Contraindicated in cerebrovascular accident

Prasugrel : This is a newer, more potent and faster acting irreversibleP2Y12 purinergic receptor blocker , as when strong antiplatelet action is required.(Option C ruled out) Like its predecessors, it is also a prodrug, but is more rapidly absorbed.(Option B ruled out) It is also more rapidly and more completely activated, resulting in faster and more consistent platelet inhibition.(Option A ruled out) Bleeding complications are also more frequent and more serious. Patients with a history of ischaemic stroke and TIAs are at greater risk of intracranial haemorrhage. Prasugrel is contraindicated in such patients. Key Takeaway: Irreversible P2Y12 blockers(Prodrugs): Clopidogrel, Ticlopidine, Prasugrel Reversible, competitive P2Y12 blocker: Cangrelor Reversible, non competitive P2Y12 blocker: Ticagrelor

Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following most accurately describes the mechanism of action of clopidogrel?

AClopidogrel directly binds to the platelet ADP receptors
BClopidogrel facilitates the action of antithrombin III
CThe active metabolite of clopidogrel binds to and inhibits the platelet ADP receptors
DThe active metabolite of clopidogrel binds to and inhibits the platelet glycoprotein IIb/IIIa receptors
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. The active metabolite of clopidogrel binds to and inhibits the platelet ADP receptors

Clopidogrel is a prodrug that is activated by CYP2C9 and CYP2C19. It irreversibly binds to the ADP receptor on the surface of platelets that serves as a key role in platelet aggregation. Reference: Basic & Clinical Pharmacology, Fourteenth Edition By Bertram G. Katzung Pg no. 834

Sample MCQ #4

A 56-year-old man undergoes a percutaneous coronary angiography procedure and placement of a stent in a coronary blood vessel. Appropriate pharmacological management will be?

AAspirin
BClopidogrel
CAspirin + Clopidogrel
DAbciximab
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Aspirin + Clopidogrel

Patient will need to be on aspirin and clopidogrel for at least a year. Aspirin and clopidogrel help prevent platelet-induced occlusion of coronary stents. Clopidogrel is a prodrug that is activated by CYP2C9 and It irreversibly binds to the ADP receptor on the surface of platelets that serves as a key role in platelet aggregation. Reference: Basic & Clinical Pharmacology, Fourteenth Edition By Bertram G. Katzung Pg no. 840-45

Sample MCQ #5

A 59-year-old man with MI is admitted for emergency percutaneous insertion of a coronary stent. Which of the following drugs might cause unexpected results based on the patient's CYP2C19 genotype?

AClopidogrel
BPrasugrel
CTicagrelor
DWarfarin
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Clopidogrel

Clopidogrel is a prodrug that must be metabolised to an active platelet-inhibiting metabolite by CYP2C19. Poor metabolizers achieve inadequate platelet inhibition. Option B & C: Prasugrel and ticagrelor do not require P450 activation and are not subject to this risk. Option D: Cytochrome P450-inducing drugs (e.g., carbamazepine, phenytoin, rifampin, barbiturates) increase warfarin's clearance and reduce the anticoagulant effect of a given dose. Cytochrome P450 inhibitors (e.g., amiodarone, selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors, cimetidine) reduce warfarin's clearance and increase the anticoagulant effect of a given dose. Genetic variability in cytochrome CYP2C9 and VKOR affect responses to warfarin. Reference : Basic & Clinical Pharmacology by Bertram G. Katzung, 14th Edition, Page Number- 79.

Sample MCQ #7

A 55-year-old man is brought 2 h after the onset of severe chest pain. He has a history of poorly controlled mild hypertension and elevated blood cholesterol but does not smoke. ECG changes of ST elevation and cardiac enzymes confirm the diagnosis of myocardial infarction. The decision is made to attempt to open his occluded artery. Which of the following drugs accelerates the conversion of plasminogen to plasmin?

AAminocaproic acid
BHeparin
CArgatroban
DReteplase
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Reteplase

Reteplase is the only thrombolytic drug listed. Heparin is anticoagulant. Argatroban is a direct inhibitor of thrombin, and Aminocaproic acid is an inhibitor, not an activator, of the conversion of plasminogen to plasmin. Reference: Basic & Clinical Pharmacology, Fourteenth Edition By Bertram G. Katzung Pg no. 836
