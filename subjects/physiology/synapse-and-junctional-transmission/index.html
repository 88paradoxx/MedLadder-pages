Sample MCQ #1

Impulses which inhibit postsynaptic neuron itself

ARenshaw cell inhibition
BPresynaptic inhibition
CDirect inhibition
DIndirect inhibition
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Renshaw cell inhibition

Renshaw cells are the inhibitory interneurons present in spinal cord Renshaw cells are excited by a motor neuron. In turn, Renshaw cells inhibit the same motor neuron which excites it. This stabilize the firing rate of the motor neurons Also called as "negative feedback inhibition" Reference: Ganong, 25 th edition page no.122

Sample MCQ #2

Increased release of Ach can occur by:

ABlocking Na+ channels on presynaptic membrane
BBlocking K+ channel on presynaptic membrane
CBlocking leaky Cl- channels on presynaptic membrane
DBlocking Ca2+ channels on presynaptic membrane
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Blocking K+ channel on presynaptic membrane

Blocking K+ channels on the presynaptic membrane leads to accumulation of positively charged potassium ions inside, leading to depolarisation. Such depolarization leads to opening of the voltage gated calcium channels and neurotransmitter release. Reference: Ganong's Review of Medical Physiology, 25th Edition, Page Number-100.

Sample MCQ #3

Release of synaptic vesicles from the presynaptic terminals is inhibited by?

APreventing depolarization of nerve terminal
BInhibition of conduction of nerve impulse
CPrevention of Ca 2 + influx
DPrevention of Na + influx
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Prevention of Ca 2 + influx

Entry of calcium ions trigger the release of neurotransmitter stored in vesicles from the presynaptic ending Factors that decrease the presynaptic calcium levels, inhibits the release of neurotransmitter Reference: Ganong, 25 th edition page no.121
