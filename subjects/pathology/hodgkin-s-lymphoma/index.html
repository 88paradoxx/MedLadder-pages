About Hodgkin's Lymphoma MCQs

Hodgkin's Lymphoma MCQs separate nodular lymphocyte-predominant disease with its popcorn cells from classical subtypes, using lacunar cells, Epstein-Barr associations, and the contrasting strength of PAX-5 staining. These are high-yield NEET PG questions for FMGE, INI-CET and NEET SS.

Sample Hodgkin's Lymphoma MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

A 20-year-old male presented with cervical lymphadenopathy. Histology of lymph node shows RS cell with vague nodule formation and background T reactive lymphocytes. The cells were positive for CD20, LCA, EMA and negative for CD 15 and CD30. Diagnosis is:

ANLPHL
BT cell rich B cell lymphoma
CNodular sclerosis Hodgkin
DCLL
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. NLPHL

This is classical description of NLPHL. (Nodular lymphocyte predominant Hodgkin lymphoma) Reference: Robbins & Cotran Pathologic Basics of Diseases 9 th Edition page No.606

Sample MCQ #2

Ebstein Barr positivity is found in which of the following types of Hodgkin's disease? i) Lymphocyte depleted ii) Mixed Cellularity iii) Lymphocyte Rich iv) Nodular Sclerosis

AOnly i, ii, and iii are correct
BOnly i and iii are correct
COnly ii and iv are correct
DAll are correct
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Only i, ii, and iii are correct

HL can be classified into two clinicopathological entities: the nodular lymphocyte predominant type (NLPHL) which represents 5% of cases and are virtually never associated with EBV, and the classical HL, which can be further subdivided in four morphological subtypes: nodular sclerosis (NSHL) which accounts for the majority of cases, mixed cellularity (MCHL), lymphocyte-rich (LRHL) and lymphocyte-depleted (LDHL). Reference: Massini, G., Siemer, D., & Hohaus, S. (2009). EBV in Hodgkin Lymphoma. Mediterranean journal of hematology and infectious diseases, 1(2), e2009013. https://doi.org/10.4084/MJHID.2009.013

Sample MCQ #3

Lymphohistocytic variant of Reed Sternberg seen in which subtype of Hodgkin lymphoma?

ANodular sclerosis
BLymphocyte rich
CLymphocyte predominant
DLymphocyte depleted
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Lymphocyte predominant

Lymphohistiocytic variants (L and H cells) with polypoid nuclei, inconspicuous nucleoli, and moderately abundant cytoplasm are characteristic of the lymphocyte predominance subtype Hodgkin lymphoma. Reference: Robbins 9th ed p 608

Sample MCQ #4

Lacunar cells is seen in which type of Hodgkins Lymphoma: September 2010

AMixed cellularity type
BLymphocyte predominant
CNodular Sclerosis Type
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Nodular Sclerosis Type

Ans. C: Nodular Sclerosis Type Reed-Sternberg cells (also known as lacunar histiocytes for certain types) are different giant cells found on light microscopy in biopsies from individuals with Hodgkin's lymphoma (aka Hodgkin's disease; a type of lymphoma), and certain other disorders. They are usually derived from B lymphocytes.

Sample MCQ #5

PAX-5 is a STRONGLY POSITIVE marker for:

ADiffuse large B-cell lymphoma
BCLASSICAL Hodgkins lymphoma
CAnaplastic lymphoma
DLymphoblastic lymphoma
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Diffuse large B-cell lymphoma

PAX-5 is a B-cell differentiation including lymphoblasts (used as a novel pan B-cell marker); also seen in R-S cells in NLPHL (+) vs. classic HL (weak). Reference: Robbins & Cotran Pathologic Basis of Diseases 10 th edition, chapter 13, pg 594

Sample MCQ #6

All of the following are true about NLPHL except

ACD15-/30
BCD 20 POSITIVE
CEBV NEGATIVE
DPoor prognosis compared to classical Hl
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Poor prognosis compared to classical Hl

NLPHL is now considered to be a distinct entity from HL. The neoplastic cells in NLPHL are called the HRS like cells or L&H cells or popcorn cells. They lack CD15 and CD 30 unlike RS cells. They are positive for CD19, 20, 45, 79a and BCL2. They run a chronic indolent course. EBV is implicated in HL and not NLPHL.
