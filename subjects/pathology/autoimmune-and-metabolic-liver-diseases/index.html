Sample MCQ #1

All of the following autoantibodies are present in type 1 autoimmune hepatitis EXCEPT

AAnti-nuclear antibodies (ANA)
BAnti soluble liver antigen/liver pancreases antigen antibodies (anti- SLA/LP)
CAnti liver kidney microsome -1 antibodies (anti-LKM-1)
DAnti smooth muscle actin antibodies (SMA)
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Anti liver kidney microsome -1 antibodies (anti-LKM-1)

Type 1, more common in middle-age and older individuals, is characterized by the presence of antinuclear (ANA), anti-smooth muscle actin (SMA), anti-mitochondrial (AMA), and anti-soluble liver antigen/ liver-pancreas antigen (anti-SLA/LP) antibodies. Type 2 auto immune hepatitis, is more common in children and is characterized by anti- liver kidney microsome-1 (lkm-1)antibodies, which are directed against cyp2d6, and anti-liver cytosol-1 (acl-1) anti bodies. Reference: Robbins Basic Pathology, 10 th ed, Pg-651

Sample MCQ #2

A 53-year-old male presents to his family physician with complaints of fever, chills, and abdominal pain. He reports that he has had past episodes of a similar nature, but has not chosen to seek treatment. He has a past medical history of ulcerative colitis and had a colectomy 5 years ago for Stage I colonic adenocarcinoma, which has been in remission. An ERCP reveals strictures in the extrahepatic bile ducts. Of the following, what is the most likely diagnosis?

ACholedocho-lithiasis
BMetastatic colonic adenocarcinoma
CPancreatic adenocarcinoma
DPrimary sclerosing cholangitis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Primary sclerosing cholangitis

The signs, symptoms, and history, combined with the results of the ERCP, are most consistent with primary sclerosing cholangitis. Primary sclerosing cholangitis (psc) is a chronic liver disease in which the bile ducts inside and outside the liver become inflamed and scarred, and eventually narrowed or blocked. When this happens, bile builds up in the liver and causes further liver damage. Reference: Robbins Basic Pathology, 10 th ed, Pg-664

Sample MCQ #3

A 41-year-old female has been followed by a physician for several years. Originally, she presented to her primary care physician with episodes of itching. Today she is being seen for fatigue. On physical examination, her physician notes that her conjunctivae are yellow. Other than mild hypothyroidism requiring hormone replacement, she has no other significant past medical history. She has no family history of liver disease. Of the following, what is her most likely diagnosis?

A&alpha;-1-antitrypsin deficiency
BGalactosemia
CPrimary sclerosing cholangitis
DPrimary biliary cirrhosis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Primary biliary cirrhosis

Primary biliary cirrhosis is most common in middle aged females, and patients will often present with pruritus due to cholestasis, which can evolve to cirrhosis of the liver (D). Jaundice is a sign of liver failure, and thus, among the choices, &alpha;-1-antitrypsin and Galactosemia could cause fatigue, but they are not commonly associated with pruritus; also, Galactosemia would affect younger ages (A, B). Primary sclerosing cholangitis is associated with ulcerative colitis (C). Primary biliary cholangitis, previously called primary biliary cirrhosis, is a chronic disease in which the bile ducts in your liver are slowly destroyed. Bile is a fluid made in your liver. It aids with digestion and helps you absorb certain vitamins. Reference: Robbins Basic Pathology, 10 th ed, Pg-664
