Sample Adenoids MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

A fire breaks out during laser vocal cord surgery. What is not to be done?

APouring sterile water
BRemoving endotracheal tube
C100% oxygen after discontinuing anaesthetic gases
DGive intravenous steroids
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. 100% oxygen after discontinuing anaesthetic gases

Oxygen will further support the fire being essential for combustion. So never use 100% oxygen when a fire breaks out. Reference - Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, pg. no - 417

Sample MCQ #2

Rhinolalia clausa occurs in all of the following except:

ANasopharyngeal polyp
BNasopharyngeal carcinoma
CCleft palate
DNasopharyngeal angiofibroma
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Cleft palate

Explanation: Rhinolalia clausa is not seen in cleft palate. Instead, a cleft palate causes velopharyngeal insufficiency , leading to hypernasal speech (rhinolalia aperta) due to excessive nasal resonance, rather than the nasal obstruction characteristic of rhinolalia clausa. Rhinolalia clausa Rhinolalia clausa or hyponasality is the lack of nasal resonance for sounds that are normally resonated in the nasal cavity, such as m, n, and ng . It manifests as a nasal or blocked voice caused by obstruction of the nasal passages or nasopharynx. Common causes of hyponasality Common cold Nasal allergy Nasal polyp Nasal growth Adenoid hypertrophy Nasopharyngeal mass (Option A, B, D ruled out)

Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following is not a method for adenoidectomy?

ACoblation
BMicrodebrider
CSuction diathermy
DDissection and snare
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Dissection and snare

Dissection and Snare method is used for tonsillectomy . The other three methods are used for adenoidectomy. These are endoscopy based newer procedures for adenoidectomy. Coblation is the best Method of Surgery. The traditional method for adenoidectomy is curettage which is a blind procedure. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, pg. no. 503, 507

Sample MCQ #4

Grisel syndrome is a possible complication of which of the following surgical procedure?

AAdenoidectomy
BTonsillectomy
CTracheostomy
DAdenotonsillectomy
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Adenotonsillectomy

It is atlanto-axial dislocation. Can follow adenoid surgery or nasopharyngeal infections. Manifests with painful neck and torticollis. Treatment includes cervical collar and even traction. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose, AND Throat by PL Dhingra, Pg.492

Sample MCQ #5

Not a true statement regarding adenoids

APhysiological enlargement continues up to 6 years of age
BAdenoids do not have capsule
CAdenoids do not have crypts
DDigital palpation of adenoid tissue should be avoided before removal in adenoidectomy
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Digital palpation of adenoid tissue should be avoided before removal in adenoidectomy

Before adenoidectomy, the adenoid mass should always be palpated before surgery to: Confirm the diagnosis To rule out any abnormal vessel overlying the adenoid mass. Adenoid is irregular on palpation &ndash; like a bag of worms. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, Pg. no. 507

Sample MCQ #6

A 52 year old female patient underwent total thyroidectomy. While surgery, both side superior laryngeal nerves got damaged accidentally. What are the expected postoperative symptoms of patient?

APoor quality of voice and aspiration
BRespiratory distress & Aspiration
CRespiratory distress with poor quality of voice
DRespiratory distress with normal voice
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Poor quality of voice and aspiration

Cricothyroid is the main tensor of the vocal cord which is supplied by external branch of SLN. Paralysis of this muscle leads to bowing down of vocal cords and hence loss of pitch of voice. The sensory supply of the larynx till vocal cords is by the internal branch of the superior laryngeal nerve. The loss of this supply can lead to aspiration. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra ,8 th Edition, pg. no &ndash; 350

Sample MCQ #7

The most likely symptom and laryngeal examination finding in a case of unilateral superior laryngeal nerve palsy are given below. Which is the correct combination?

AAphonia; vocal cord in cadaveric position
BWeak voice and mild aspiration; bowing of affected vocal cord
CSevere aspiration without voice change; fully mobile vocal cords
DAsymptomatic; posterior commissure deviation to normal side
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Weak voice and mild aspiration; bowing of affected vocal cord

UNILATERAL SLN PALSY Anaesthesia of the supraglottic larynx on affected side - may pass unnoticed or cause occasional aspiration. Paralysis of cricothyroid on affected side &ndash; weak voice and pitch cannot be raised with decreased ability to sing. Bowing of affected vocal cord as cricothyroid is the main tensor of larynx. Askew position of glottis as anterior commissure is rotated to the healthy side. Reference &ndash; Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra ,8 th Edition, pg. no &ndash; 350
