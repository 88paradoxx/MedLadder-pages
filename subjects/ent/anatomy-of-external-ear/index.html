Sample Anatomy of External Ear MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

A patient with ear wax underwent syringing. During syringing, the patient developed a syncopal episode. Stimulation of which nerve is responsible?

AAuriculotemporal nerve
BChorda Tympani nerve
CArnold's nerve
DJacobson's nerve
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Arnold's nerve

Auricular branch of the vagus (CN X), also called Arnold's nerve, supplies the concha and corresponding eminence on the medial surface. Vagus nerve has a parasympathetic action on the muscle fibers of the heart, so its stimulation during syringing of the ear canal can cause syncopal episode in some patients. Other Options: By stimulation of the other three nerves given in other three options don't cause syncope on stimulation.

Sample MCQ #2

Which of the following best describes Fissure of Santorini

ANatural deficiency in cartilaginous part of external auditory canal
BNatural deficiency in bony part of external auditory canal
CNatural deficiency in fallopian canal of facial nerve
DVertical bony septum in Internal Auditory Canal
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Natural deficiency in cartilaginous part of external auditory canal

Fissures of Santorini are natural dehiscence in cartilaginous part of EAC. They allow transmission of infection and malignancy from EAC to Parotid gland Reference : Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8th Edition, Chapter name: Anatomy of Ear, Page number:3

Sample MCQ #3 · Image-based

Match the following:

A1-D, 2-C, 3-A, 4-B
B1-C, 2-A, 3-B, 4-D
C1-C, 2-B, 3-A, 4-D
D1-D, 2-A, 3-B, 4-C
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. 1-C, 2-A, 3-B, 4-D

Explanation:

  1. Greater auricular nerve
    • This nerve provides sensation to the ear lobe and posterior auricle.
    • So, the correct match is Skin over the ear lobe and posterior auricle.
  2. Auriculotemporal nerve
    • This nerve, a branch of the mandibular nerve (V3), provides sensation to the external ear and the temporal region.
    • So, the correct match is External ear and temporal region.
  3. Auricular branch of the vagus nerve
    • Also known as Arnold's nerve, it provides sensation to the external acoustic meatus and auricle.
    • So, the correct match is External acoustic meatus and auricle.
  4. Lesser occipital nerve
    • This nerve provides sensation to the posterior auricle and the skin over the occipital region.
    • So, the correct match is Skin over occipital region.
