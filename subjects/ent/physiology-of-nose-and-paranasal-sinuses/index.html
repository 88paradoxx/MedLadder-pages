Sample MCQ #1

Which of the following is the best test for olfaction?

ASniffing of clove oil
BUPSIT
CAcoustic rhinomanometry
DSniffing of ammonia
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. UPSIT

University of Pennsylvania Smell Identification Test or UPSIT Best test for olfaction. Can be self-administered in 10 to 15 minutes. Incorrect Options: Option A - Clove oil is not a standardized test of olfaction. Option C - Acoustic rhinomanometry records the total air flow and resistance to air flow. It is not a test of olfaction. Option D - Ammonia is an irritant and does not stimulate the olfactory receptors. Reference: Scott-Brown's Otolaryngology, Head and Neck Surgery: Volume 1, 8th Edition, Page Number 1232, 991; Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, Page Number-164.

Sample MCQ #2

Which structure cannot be approached through transnasal endoscopic surgery?

ALacrimal Sac
BCribriform plate
CLacrimal gland
DOptic nerve
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Lacrimal gland

Think about the anatomy of the eye - lacrimal glands lie in lateral part of orbit . So cannot be approached via transnasal route. Endoscopic surgery can be used to access the orbit, optic nerve, lacrimal sac and pituitary gland in addition to the nose and paranasal sinuses. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, pg. no 494 Parson's Diseases of the Eye - 23 rd edition - Page - 422, 423 Scott Brown's Otolaryngology, Head and Neck Surgery, 8 th edition - Volume 1 - Page - 1244, 1283

Sample MCQ #3

The odour receptor is present in:

ANeurons of olfactory epithelium
BOlfactory tract
CAmygdala
DOlfactory bulbs
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Neurons of olfactory epithelium

Smell is perceived in the olfactory region of the nose, which is situated high up in the nasal cavity (upper 1/3 rd of nasal cavity). Olfactory area is also called the dangerous area of the nose. Esthesioneuroblastoma (Olfactory Neuroblastoma) arises from the olfactory epithelium. Olfactory pathway: Neurons of olfactory epithelium &rarr; Olfactory nerves &rarr; Pass through the cribriform plate &rarr; Mitral cells of the olfactory bulb &rarr; Olfactory tract &rarr; Prepyriform cortex and amygdaloid nucleus. Olfactory system is also associated with the autonomic system at the hypothalamic level. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, Page Number-164, 239.

This is a case of Kallmann syndrome . Anosmia Congenital hypogonadism leading to delay in puberty Reference &ndash; Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra , 8 th Edition, pg. no &ndash; 547
