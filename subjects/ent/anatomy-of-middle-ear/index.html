Sample Anatomy of Middle Ear MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

Not a true match about the anatomical aspects of ear

AKorner's septum is remnant of petrosquamous suture
BSubiculum is a bony spicule which separates oval and round windows
CProcessus Cochleariformis indicates the basal turn of cochlea.
DCanal for Tensor tympani lies on the anterior wall of middle ear
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Processus Cochleariformis indicates the basal turn of cochlea.

Processus cochleariformis is a hook like process on the medial wall of middle ear which marks the first genu of facial nerve. Tendon of tensor tympani takes a turn here to attach to malleus. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8th Edition, Page - 5,6, 9

Sample MCQ #2

Infection of CNS spreads to tympanic cavity(=middle ear) through:

ACochlear aqueduct
BVestibular aqueduct
CCanal Of Huguier
DHyrtl's fissure
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Hyrtl's fissure

Hyrtl's fissure connects middle ear to middle cranial fossa. Canal of Huguier is the exit point of chorda tympani nerve from middle ear Vestibular aqueduct is the bony cover of Endolymphatic duct Aqueduct of cochlea connects inner ear to CSF in subarachnoid space of brain(not middle ear) Reference : Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8th Edition, Chapter name: Some Memorable Nuggets for Rapid Review, Page number: 547

Sample MCQ #3

Which of the following middle ear structure develops from neural crest cells?

AMalleus
BStapes
CFoot plate of stapes
DIncus
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Foot plate of stapes

Explanation: Middle ear ossicles begin forming around 6 weeks of development as cartilaginous structures . Malleus and Incus: Derived from mesoderm of the first pharyngeal arch . Stapes: Suprastructure: From mesoderm of the second pharyngeal arch . Footplate: From the otic capsule , which is derived from neural crest cells .
