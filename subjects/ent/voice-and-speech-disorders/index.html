Sample MCQ #2

Ideal treatment for Reinke's Oedema

AAntibiotics and steroids
BSteroids only
CSpeech therapy
DDecortication of cords
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Decortication of cords

Reinke's oedema is due to oedema of the subepithelial space of the vocal cord (Reinke's space). TREATMENT- Decortication of the vocal cord (removal of strip of epithelium is done on one side and 3 - 4weeks on the other). Voice rest, speech therapy, cessation of smoking Reference - Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, pg. no - 342

Sample MCQ #3

All are true about Reinke's odema except

AKnown as Smoker's larynx
BPresents with hoarseness
CSpeech therapy and Proton Pump Inhibitor are the mainstay of treatment
DSub-epithelial cordectomy is the treatment of choice
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Speech therapy and Proton Pump Inhibitor are the mainstay of treatment

Under the epithelium of vocal cords is a potential space with scanty subepithelial connective tissues. It is bounded above and below by the arcuate lines, in front by anterior commissure, and behind by vocal process of arytenoid. Oedema of this space causes fusiform swelling of the membranous cords (Reinke&rsquo;s oedema). Stopping smoking and Subepithelial cordectomy is the treatment of choice. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose, AND Throat by PL Dhingra, Pg.342

Sample MCQ #4

Type of voice in adenoid hypertrophy is:

ARhinolalia aperta
BRhinolalia clausa
CHot-potato voice
DDysphonia Plica ventricularis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Rhinolalia clausa

Rhinolalia clausa is hyponasality. It is due to blockage of the nose or nasopharynx. Common causes include common cold, nasal polyp, sinusitis, adenoid hypertrophy, nasopharyngeal mass. Rhinolalia aperta is hypernasality - Seen in cleft palate, palatal perforation, palatal palsy Hot potato voice is seen in peritonsillar abscess(quinsy). Dysphonia Plica ventricularis - Here voice is produced by false vocal cords. May be secondary to impaired function of the true vocal cords like paralysis, surgical excision or cancer. Reference: Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, Pg. no. 366, 368 , 311

Sample MCQ #6

Treatment of choice of vocal polyp is :

AVoice rest
BMicrolaryngoscopic removal
CCryotherapy
DSpeech therapy
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Microlaryngoscopic removal

Vocal polyp is caused by sudden shouting resulting in haemorrhage in the vocal cord and subsequent submucosal oedema. Treatment is surgical excision under operating microscope followed by speech therapy. Reference- Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, pg. no - 353

Sample MCQ #7

Laryngopharyngeal reflux can lead to all except

ASulcus vocalis
BPachydermia laryngis
CVocal Nodules
DPseudosulcus vocalis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Sulcus vocalis

Sulcus vocalis Furrow like depression in vocal cord Due to deficiency of lamina propria, the epithelium dips down forming a furrow. Can be congenital or due to phonotrauma. Laryngopharyngeal reflux causes pseudosulcus vocalis. Reference - Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra, 8 th Edition, pg. no - 355 Scott Brown&rsquo;s Otolaryngology, Head and Neck Surgery &ndash; 8 th edition &ndash; Volume 3 &ndash; Page number &ndash; 1095

Sample MCQ #8

Most common cause of bilateral vocal cord palsy

ACarcinoma bronchus
BIdiopathic
CThyroid surgery
DCarcinoma Thyroid
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Thyroid surgery

Thyroid surgery (Total thyroidectomy) is the most common cause of bilateral vocal cord palsy. Most common cause of unilateral vocal cord palsy is idiopathic followed by carcinoma bronchus. Reference &ndash; Diseases of Ear, Nose and Throat & Head and Neck Surgery by P L Dhingra ,8 th Edition, pg. no 348 REFERENCE- Scott Brown&rsquo;s Otolaryngology, Head and Neck Surgery &ndash; 8 th edition &ndash; Volume 3 &ndash; Page number &ndash; 1108
