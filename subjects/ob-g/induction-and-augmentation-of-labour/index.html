Sample MCQ #1

A female at 37 weeks of gestation has mild labour pains for 10 hours and cervix is persistently 1 cm dilated but non effaced. What will be the next appropriate management?

ASedation and wait
BAugmentation with oxytocin
CCesarean section
DAmniotomy
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Sedation and wait

The patient in the question has been having mild labor pains for 10 hours with no change in cervical dilatation and effacement. But again the question does not specify whether she is having regular uterine contractions or not. False labor can be differentiated from latent phase of labor by therapeutic rest i.eThe patient is sedated with morphine. So the correct option is Sedate and Wait. As far as augmentation with oxytocin is concerned - it is done only in those patients who even after therapeutic rest continue to be in prolonged latent phase (once the diagnosis of lack of progress of latent phase is confirmed following therapeutic rest.) Amniotomy is not useful and should be avoided in patients in prolonged latent phase. Also, there is no indication of cesarean section in this case. Reference : Fernando Arias 3/e, pg 376

Sample MCQ #2

All of the following drugs are effective for cervical ripening during pregnancy except:

AProstaglandin E2
BOxytocin
CProgesterone
DMisoprostol
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Progesterone

Ripening of the cervix is changing the cervical matrix from sol to gel state by dissolving the collagen bundles. Ultimately the cervix becomes soft. Techniques for cervical ripening: Pharmacological: Dinoprostone gel (PGE 2 ) - it is the gold standard for cervical ripening Misoprostol (PGE1) tablet - vaginal or oral Steroid receptor antagonist Mifepristone Oxytocin Non pharmacological: Stripping the membrane Mechanical dilators: Osmotic dilators Balloon catheter/Transcervical catheter placed through internal os Extra-amniotic saline infusion Reference : Dutta Obs. 7/e, pg 524; Fernando Arias 3/e, pg 284-286; Williams Obs. 24/e, pg 525

Sample MCQ #3

Prophylactic methergin given for ?

AInduction of labour
BInduction of abortion
CTo stop excess bleeding from uterus
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. To stop excess bleeding from uterus

Ans, C. To stop excess bleeding from uterus Methergin (methyl-ergo-novine) is a semisynthetic ergot derivative derived from lysergic acid. Indications Prophylactic:- Active management of 3rd stage of labour to prevent excess bleeding following delivery. Therapeutic:- To stop atonic uterine bleeding.

Sample MCQ #4

Which one of the following methods for induction of labour should not be used in patients with previous lower segment cesarean section?

AProstaglandin gel
BProstaglandin tablet
CStripping of the membrane
DOxytocin drip
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Prostaglandin tablet

Misoprostol It is an analogue of Prostaglandin E 1 . Indications: Medical abortion in the first trimester of pregnancy. Evacuation of uterus in case of anembryonic pregnancies or early fetal demise. Ripening of cervix prior to second trimester abortions. Ripening of cervix and induction of labour in term pregnancies. For treatment and prevention of postpartum bleeding. Pharmacokinetics: It is available in tablet form. Metabolized in the liver. Complication: Nausea, vomiting, diarrhea. Uterine hyperstimulation. Contraindication: Women with previous uterine surgery particularly cesarean section. Reference : Dutta Obs. 7/e, pg 524; Fernando Arias 3/e, pg 286
