Sample MCQ #3

Feto maternal transfusion is detected by:

AKleihauer test
BSpectrophotometry
CBenzidine test
DNone of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Kleihauer test

Kleihauer-Betke test It is performed on maternal blood to assess the amount of fetomaternal bleed. In order to calculate the dose of Anti-D prophylaxis required. Principle: HbF is more resistant to acid elution than HbA. The maternal blood is subjected to an acid solution (citric acid phosphate buffer). Acid will elute the adult hemoglobins but not the fetal haemoglobin from the red cells. Hence, the fetal red cells appear stained dark red, unlike the light-coloured maternal red cells or ghost cells. The number of fetal red cells in 50 low power fields is assessed. Also Know Indirect Coombs test - detects antibodies in maternal serum. Direct Coombs test - detects antibodies in fetus/neonate. Reference : Dutta Obs. 7/e, pg 334, Sheila Balakrishnan Textbook of Obs 1/e, pg 368.

Sample MCQ #4

Consequence of maternal use of cocaine is:

AHydrops
BSacral agenesis
CCerebral infarction
DHypertrichosis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Cerebral infarction

It can lead to the following Malformations in the fetus: Microcephaly, cutis aplasia, porencephaly, subependymal and periventricular cysts, ileal atresia, cardiac anomalies, visceral infarcts, limb reduction defects and genitourinary malformations. It produces cerebral infarction and periventricular leukomalacia in the fetus. The risk of malformations in the embryo/fetus is highest after the first trimester. Reference : Sheila Balakrishnan, pg 696; Williams Obs. 22/e, pg 364, 23/e, pg 326, 327

Sample MCQ #6

What is approx fetal weight, if height of uterus is above pubic symphysis is 35 cm and station of head is +2?

A2.5 kg
B3 kg
C3.5 kg
D4 kg
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. 3.5 kg

Estimation of fetal weight can be done using Johnson formula: If station of head below ischial spine [Height of uterus above pubic symphysis (in cm) - 11] &times; 155 If fetal head is at or above ischial spine- [Height of uterus above pubic symphysis (in cm) - 12] &times; 155 Here fetal head is at + 2, i.e., above ischial spine, so it will be (35 - 12) &times; 155 = 3.5 kg. Reference: Dutta Obs. 8/e, p 84

AWell-flexed head
BEfficient uterine contraction

DTone of the abdominal muscles

✓ Correct Answer: D. Tone of the abdominal muscles

Factors facilitating descent are: (1) uterine contraction and retraction, (2) bearing down efforts and (3) straightening of the fetal ovoid specially after rupture of the membranes. Reference : Dutta Obs. 7/e, pg 125

Sample MCQ #8

All of the following are true about anencephaly except:

ACan be diagnosed in 1st trimester
BIncreased alpha-fetoprotein
CEnlarged adrenal gland
DPolyhydramnios
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Enlarged adrenal gland

Anencephaly is characterized by absence of the cranium and telencephalic structures, with the skull base and orbits covered only by angiomatous stroma. Acrania is the absence of the cranium, with protrusion of disorganized brain tissue. Both are generally grouped together, and anencephaly is considered to be the final stage of acrania. Reference: Fernando Arias 3/e, p 64; Dutta Obs. 7/e, p 408, 409
