Sample MCQ #1

A 22-year-old female with history of multiple contacts presenting with growth on vulva, the probable diagnosis is:

ACondyloma accuminata
BVerruca plana
CVerruca vulgaris
DCondyloma lata
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Condyloma accuminata

History of sexual exposure and growths on vulva is consistent with condyloma accuminata. Condylomata acuminata (singular: condyloma acuminatum) refers to anogenital warts caused by human papillomavirus (HPV). The most common strains of HPV that cause anogenital warts are 6 and 11 Condyloma accuminata : Warts are common in the regions affected most directly by coitus i.e. posterior fourchette and lateral areas of vulva. These verrucous growths may coalesce to form large cauliflower growths. Condyloma is associated with vulval, vaginal, and cervical cancers. Reference: Shaw 15th/ed pp 138, 139

Sample MCQ #3

In which of the following heart diseases maternal mortality is found to be highest?

A.Eisenmenger's complex
BCoarctation of aorta
CMitral stenosis
DAortic stenosis
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. .Eisenmenger's complex

Eisenmenger's syndrome is the presence of secondary pulmonary hypertension that develops from any cardiac lesion. The syndrome develops when increased pulmonary blood flow due to left to right shunt produces a right side pressure more than the left side and hence reversal of shunt occurs and subsequently cyanosis develops. It is the heart disease with the worst prognosis during pregnancy with a maternal mortality of 50%. Hence, pregnancy is contraindicated in patients of eisenmengers. If diagnosis of Eisenmenger is made in the first trimester, termination of pregnancy is advised. Most common cause of death in Eisenmenger's syndrome is right ventricular failure with cardiogenic shock. Reference: Williams Obs. 22/e, p 1028, 23/e, p 970; Sheila Balakrishnan, p 283

Sample MCQ #4

A 25 year old primigravida with a 10 week pregnancy patient comes to an antenatal clinic complaining of an increase in urinary frequency, which of the following can cause increased urinary frequency in pregnancy?

AFailure of retroversion of gravid uterus
BCongestion of bladder mucosa
CIncreased thirst in pregnancy
DAll of the above
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. All of the above

Frequency of micturition is quite a troublesome symptom during 8-12th weeks of pregnancy. It is due to Resting of the bulky uterus on the fundus of the bladder because of exaggerated anteverted position of the uterus, Congestion of the bladder mucosa and Change in maternal osmoregulation causing increased thirst and polyuria. As the uterus straightens up after the 12th week, the symptoms disappear. Reference: DC Dutta textbook of obstetrics, 8th edition; chapter 7

Sample MCQ #6

Peak in cardiac output antenatally occurs at

A28-30 wk
B20-22 wk
C32-34 wk
D36-38 wk
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. 28-30 wk

Cardiac output increases significantly during pregnancy to meet the increased metabolic demands of both the mother and the developing fetus. This increase begins early in pregnancy and peaks around 28-30weeks gestation. During this time, the volume of blood returning to the heart (venous return) increases due to increased blood volume and the effect of the growing uterus on venous return. Additionally, the cardiac output is supported by elevated heart rate and stroke volume. After reaching its peak around 28-30 weeks, cardiac output may slightly decline as the pregnancy approaches term, but it generally remains elevated compared to pre-pregnancy levels Reference : Arias 5 th edition pg no 177
