Sample Disorders of Menstruation MCQs with answers

Sample MCQ #1

A 48-year-old woman presents with heavy menstrual bleeding. Which of the following hormones is most commonly associated with shedding of the endometrium during menstruation?

ALuteinizing hormone
BFollicle-stimulating hormone
CProgesterone
DEstrogen
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Progesterone

Involution of the corpus luteum results in a decrease in progesterone and estradiol secretion. This occurs around day 24 of a 28-day menstrual cycle. The loss of hormonal support causes endometrial shedding. LH, FSH, GnRH and estrogen are not involved in this process. Estradiol is primarily responsible for endometrial proliferation during the earlier phases of menstrual cycle. Reference : Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine 21 st edition Page 3035

Sample MCQ #2

A girl with primary amenorrhea with normal ovaries, absent internal genitalia with normal external genitalia. Probable diagnosis?

AMayer-Rokitansky-Kuster-Hauser syndrome
BTurner's syndrome
CNoonan's
DAndrogen insensitivity syndrome
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Mayer-Rokitansky-Kuster-Hauser syndrome

The question says that the girl has normal ovaries which rules out. Turners syndrome where streak gonads are seen Noonan's syndrome Androgen insensitivity syndrome gonads are testis. So by exclusion our answer is Mayer-Rokitansky Kuster-Hauser Syndrome. Mullerian agenesis or mayer-okintansky-Kuster-hauser syndrome is a congenital malformation characterized by a failure of the Mullerian ducts to develop, resulting in absent uterus and variable malformations of the vagina, while both ovaries are intact and functioning. Ovaries are present and function normally so secondary sexual characteristics are normal. External genitalia are also normal as testosterone is absent. Reference : Speroff 7th/ed p 420-421.

Sample MCQ #3

Primary amenorrhea with absent uterus, normal breasts and scanty pubic hair is seen in:

AMayer Rokitansky Kuster hauser Syndrome
BTurner Syndrome
CNoonan Syndrome
DTesticular feminisation syndrome
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Testicular feminisation syndrome

The patient is presenting with primary amenorrhea along with well developed breast and scanty pubic hair and axillary hair -leave no doubt regarding Testicular feminisation syndrome as the diagnosis, which c/k/a AIS (Androgen Insensitivity Syndrome) Reference : Novak 14 th /ed, pg no: 1037-1038

Sample MCQ #4

A 16 year old child with primary amenorrhea with negative progesterone challenge test but positive combined progesterone and estrogen test. Diagnosis may be:

AMullerian agenesis
BPCOD
CAsherman syndrome
DProlactinoma
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: D. Prolactinoma

This child is presenting with primary amenorrhea with: Negative progesterone challenge test - which rules out PCOD (which may sometimes manifest as primary amenorrhea) When next step was done i.e., estrogen, progesterone combined test - lt comes out to be positive i.e., compartment I system (uterus, endometrium and outflow tract) is normal if properly stimulated by estrogen which rules out mullerian agenesis and Ashermann's syndrome. Positive estrogen progesterone combined test means the defect is in the production of estrogen i.e., either ovaries, pituitary, or hypothalamus. So from the given options we have to look for a cause which involves either of the above sites, which in this case is prolactinoma. Reference : Dutta Gynae 6 th /ed pg no: 467.

Sample MCQ #5

Which is not primary amenorrhea?

ASheehan's syndrome
BKallmann's syndrome
CMayer Rokitansky Koster Hauser syndrome
DTurner syndrome
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Sheehan's syndrome

Kallmann&rsquo;s syndrome is due to hypothalamic dysfunction characterised by a deficiency of gonadotropin releasing hormone (GnRH) causing a hypogonadotropic hypogonadism. This is associated with anosmia. It can occasionally be associated with optic problems, such as color blindness or optic atrophy, nerve deafness, cleft palate, cryptorchidism, renal agenesis, and mirror movement disorder. MRKH syndrome, also known as Mullerian agenesis, is due to anatomical absence of the uterus. This is the second most common cause of primary amenorrhea Turner&rsquo;s syndrome is a type of gonadal dysgenesis and is overall the most common cause of primary amenorrhea Sheehan&rsquo;s syndrome is postpartum pituitary necrosis. It leads to secondary amenorrhea and not primary amenorrhea. Reference: Shaw 15 th /ed, Page no:96-97

Pubertal events in girls which are not estrogen dependent?

AMenstruation
BVaginal cornification
CHeight spurt
DHair growth

The main hormone responsible for secondary sexual characteristics in females is estrogen. Estrogen leads to Breast development Growth spurt, i.e. height attained Production of cervical mucus Cornification of vaginal cells Menstruation (menstruation occurs due to withdrawal of progesterone in an oestrogen primed uterus). As far as hair growth is concerned &ndash; in females also the hormone responsible is Androgens (produced by adrenals and ovary) Reference: Williams Gynae 1st/ed p 315; Novak 14th/ed p 992, 15th/ed p 993

Sample MCQ #7

All are used in treating spasmodic dysmenorrhea except:

ABromocriptine
BIbuprofen
CMefenamic acid
DNorethisterone and ethinyl estradiol
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Bromocriptine

Spasmodic dysmenorrhoea is another name for primary dysmenorrhoea. The etiology of primary dysmenorrhea includes excessive amount of prostanoids secreted from the endometrium during menstruation. The prostanoids result in increased uterine contractions with a dysrhythmic pattern, increased basal tone, and increased active pressure. Pain is spasmodic in nature Pain of primary dysmenorrhea usually begins a few hours before or just after the onset of a menstrual period and may last 48 to 72 hours. Characteristics of spasmodic dysmenorrhoea Seen in adolescent girls Pain begins few hours before a rest of menstruation and lasts for 12&ndash;24 hours, but never 48 hours more than Management: NSAIDs, OCP&rsquo;s can be used for management. Reference: Berek & Novak's gynecology 16th ed, p571

Sample MCQ #8

17-year-old girl with height of 58 inches present with primary amenorrhea and rising FSH. The histological finding most consistent with her conditions is:

ALow oocyte in ovary
BCorpus luteum hemorrhage
CMarfan syndrome
DPituitary apoplexy
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Low oocyte in ovary

A height of 58 inches corresponds to approximately 147 cm. Which is short stature for a female of 16 years. Her FSH levels are rising, i.e., estrogen production is low i.e. there is an ovarian cause for 1&deg; amenorrhea. Primary amenorrhea with short stature is seen in Turners syndrome. Turner syndrome (45 X) is the most common chromosomal abnormality causing gonadal failure, hence on histological examination their ovary should low oocyte count. These patients present with short stature.
