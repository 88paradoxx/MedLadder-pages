Sample MCQ #1

Where is the SRY region located in the body?

AShort arm of Y chromosome
BLong arm of Y chromosome
CShort arm of X chromosome
DLong arm of X chromosome
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Short arm of Y chromosome

Explanation: The SRY gene/region is the sex-determining gene/region located on the distal region of the short arm of the Y chromosome . It has a significant role in determining male sex and expresses at the 5th to 6th week of development. In XX karyotype the absence of this gene expression leads to ovarian development. Mutation of the SRY gene leads to Swyer syndrome. Patients with Swyer syndrome have a 46XY karyotype , but their Mullerian system develops normally. These individuals are phenotypically female with both female external and internal genitalia. Their gonads are usually streak ovaries composed only of gonadal stroma, while the vagina, uterus, and fallopian tubes are all formed. Reference: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8840551/#:~:text=Abstract,testis%20from%20an%20undifferentiated%20gonad.

Sample MCQ #2

In cases of gonadal sex reversal syndrome with an XX karyotype, what is the most common underlying cause?

APoint mutation
BDeletion
CTranslocation
DNon-sense mutation
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: C. Translocation

Explanation: In gonadal sex reversal syndrome with an XX karyotype, the most common cause is a translocation . Translocation is the rearrangement of chromosomal material between non-homologous chromosomes. Sex reversal syndrome, also known as disorder of sex development (DSD), arises when an individual's chromosomal, gonadal, and phenotypic sex do not align. In gonadal sex reversal syndrome with an XX karyotype, a translocation involving the SRY (Sex-determining Region Y) gene can cause the gene to be present on an X chromosome. Because the SRY gene initiates male development, its translocation to the X chromosome can cause individuals with an XX karyotype to develop testes rather than ovaries. Reference: https://afju.springeropen.com/articles/10.1186/s12301-021-00210-5

Sample MCQ #3

In Precocious puberty age limit of girls is-

A8 year
B10 year
C9 year
D11 year
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. 8 year

Answer- A. 8 years Precocious puberty in a girl is the appearance of any of the secondary sexual characteristics before the age of 8 years or the occurrence of menarche before the age of 10 years. Precocious puberty in boys is the onset of secondary sexual characteristics before the age of nine years.

Sample MCQ #4

Which of the following statements is not true regarding bartholin gland?

AIt is situated in superficial perineal pouch
BBartholin's gland is about 2 cm and duct measures 0.5 cm
CBartholin duct is lined by columnar epithelium
DIt secrets mucus which is alkaline in nature
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Bartholin's gland is about 2 cm and duct measures 0.5 cm

Bartholin&rsquo;s gland is situated in the superficial perineal pouch and measures about 0.5 cm. The duct measures 2 cm. The gland is compound racemose and lined by columnar epithelium. During sexual excitement, it secretes abundant alkaline mucus which helps in lubrication. Duct opens outside the hymen at the junction of the anterior two-third and posterior one-third in the groove between the hymen and the labium minus. The duct is lined by columnar epithelium except near the opening, where it is lined by stratified squamous epithelium. The Bartholin&rsquo;s gland corresponds to the bulbourethral gland of male. Reference: D C Dutta&rsquo;s textbook of gynecology, 7th edition, chapter 1

All of the following take part in male genital tract development except:

ASRY
BSOX-9
CFGF-9
DWNT-4

✓ Correct Answer: D. WNT-4

Genes for male development SRY gene-Master Gene for testis development SOX 9-SRY & SOX 9 induce testis to form FGF-9 (chemotactic factor) FGF-9 causes tubules from mesonephric duct to penetrate gonadal ridge SF1 (steroidogenic factor)-It stimulates differentiation of sertole or leydig cells WNT-4 is an ovary-determining gene. Sample MCQ #6

What is the cause for luteal phase defect?

AProgesterone is inadequately secreted
BExcess estrogen is secreted
CExcess progesterone is secreted
DEstrogen defect
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: A. Progesterone is inadequately secreted

Luteal Phase Defect During normal luteal phase, when there is adequate progesterone secretion by the corpus luteum, adequate development of secretory endometrium occurs for blastocyst implantation. Luteal phase defect refers to a condition when production of progesterone is suboptimal by corpus luteum. Inadequate progesterone secretion could be due to: Inadequate follicular development Inadequate FSH or LH secretion Hyperprolactinemia Reference: Novak 14 th /ed p 1225, 15 th /ed p 1161

Sample MCQ #8

GnRH analogue may be given in all of the following except:

AProstate Ca
BEndometrial Ca
CFibromyoma - uterus
DPrecocious puberty
Show Answer & Explanation ▾
✓ Correct Answer: B. Endometrial Ca

Indications for using Synthetic GnRH In females: (In all those conditions where there is increased estrogen, GnRH analogues are useful). In males: Cryptorchidism: short-term intranasal nafarelin is used. Carcinoma prostate: GnRH agonists are as effective as orchidectomy in Ca prostate but if there are neurological symptoms or life-threatening metastatic disease - androgens should first be started followed by GnRH agonist. BPH: Daily administration of leuprorelin or nafarelin decreases obstructive urinary symptoms in 1-6 monthstime. Reference: Jeffcoate 7 th /ed p 652
