Free All-India Mock Tests for NEET PG, INI-CET & FMGE

MedLadder's live mock tests recreate the real exam hall online. Every scheduled paper follows the actual exam pattern — same number of questions, same duration and the same marking scheme, including negative marking. You write the paper in a timed interface with a question palette, mark-for-review and auto-submit, just like the real thing.

How a live mock test works

  1. Join free. Sign in and register for any upcoming mock — scheduled All-India tests cost nothing.
  2. Write the paper. The test goes live at the scheduled time. Answer, skip, mark and navigate freely until you submit or time runs out.
  3. Get your All-India rank. Grading happens on the server the moment you submit. Your rank appears instantly on the live leaderboard, updated in real time as others finish.
  4. Review everything. After submission, walk through every question with the correct answer and a detailed explanation.

Why take mock tests on MedLadder

Ranks here are earned, not estimated — scoring is server-side, ties are broken by completion time, and the leaderboard is the same one every participant sees. Regular Grand Tests through the year let you benchmark your preparation against thousands of aspirants across India, spot weak subjects early, and build exam-day temperament before the real NEET PG, INI-CET or FMGE.

Mock tests for USMLE, PLAB, MRCS & more

Beyond NEET PG, MedLadder schedules mock tests for USMLE Step 1 and Step 2 CK, PLAB, INI-CET, FMGE, MRCS and MRCP. USMLE-style papers use long clinical vignettes and timed blocks like the real exam; PLAB and MRCS papers focus on clinical decision-making and surgical principles respectively. Whatever your target exam, the formula is the same: a realistic paper, server-side grading, an instant rank and full answer review.
