Frequently Asked Questions

How many clinical cases are there on MedLadder?

MedLadder has 544 clinical cases across 13 body systems and 315 topics: 238 Core and 306 Advanced. 104 are free to play.

Are the clinical cases free?

Every body system has 8 free cases — 104 in total — that play without a subscription. The rest need MedLadder Pro, which also unlocks the full QBank and past papers.

What is the difference between Core and Advanced cases?

Core cases cover the presentations every NEET PG and FMGE candidate must manage. Advanced cases are written at INI-CET and NEET SS difficulty, with harder differentials, exceptions and second-line decisions.

Which exams are the clinical cases for?

NEET PG, INI-CET and FMGE first, and they work for NEET SS and USMLE-style practice too. Each case is a patient vignette where you choose the next step before the explanation and learning point appear.
