Frequently Asked Questions

How many Gastro & Hepatobiliary clinical cases are there on MedLadder?

MedLadder has 66 Gastro & Hepatobiliary clinical cases: 43 Core and 23 Advanced. 8 of them are free to play, and the remaining 58 need MedLadder Pro.

Are Gastro & Hepatobiliary clinical cases free?

8 Gastro & Hepatobiliary cases are free to play without a subscription. The other 58 cases, including all Advanced cases beyond the free set, need MedLadder Pro, which also unlocks the full QBank and past papers.

Which exams are these Gastro & Hepatobiliary clinical cases designed for?

NEET PG, INI-CET and FMGE first of all - and they work just as well for NEET SS and USMLE-style practice. Every one of these exams now tests you with patient vignettes instead of one-line facts, so each case here is built the same way: a patient story that unfolds step by step, and you commit to a diagnosis and a plan before the next clue appears.

Are these cases based on the new exam pattern?

Yes. Look at recent NEET PG, INI-CET, FMGE and NEET SS papers - and USMLE-style questions - and you will see the shift: fewer one-liners, more real patients. The question gives you a story and asks what you would do next. That is exactly how every case on this page works: history, examination, investigations and management, revealed one step at a time.

How do I use these cases to prepare for NEET PG?

Take one case at a time and decide your answer before you reveal the next step - that is how the exam will test you. Start with the Core cases for the presentations you must know, then move to Advanced when you want the harder differentials. The 8 free cases let you try the format before you decide on Pro.

Do the cases explain the reasoning, or just give the answer?

They show the reasoning. You get the history, examination, investigations and management step by step, with the key learning point at the end - so you understand why each decision was made, not just what the answer was.
