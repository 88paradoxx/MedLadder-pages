What these endocrine cases test

Endocrine cases follow the hormone through to treatment. You run DKA in the right order, holding insulin while the potassium is low, give hypertonic saline for hyponatraemia with seizures, treat adrenal crisis before the cortisol result comes back, and rule out familial hypocalciuric hypercalcaemia before sending a patient for parathyroid surgery. Advanced cases cover the dawn phenomenon versus Somogyi, MODY, Charcot foot, SGLT2-associated euglycaemic DKA and factitious hypoglycaemia.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many Endocrine & Metabolic clinical cases are there on MedLadder?

MedLadder has 39 Endocrine & Metabolic clinical cases: 15 Core and 24 Advanced. 8 of them are free to play, and the remaining 31 need MedLadder Pro.

Are Endocrine & Metabolic clinical cases free?

8 Endocrine & Metabolic cases are free to play without a subscription. The other 31 cases, including all Advanced cases beyond the free set, need MedLadder Pro, which also unlocks the full QBank and past papers.

Which endocrine topics come up most in NEET PG?

Diabetic emergencies, thyroid disorders, adrenal disease, calcium disorders, pituitary tumours and sodium problems such as SIADH and diabetes insipidus. The cases cover diagnosis, then the first and next steps in management.

Do the cases cover diabetes management in depth?

Yes. They cover DKA and HHS, hypoglycaemia, insulin adjustment, monogenic diabetes and diabetic foot complications, with explanations of the thresholds the exam uses.

Are there cases on endocrine tumours?

Yes. They cover phaeochromocytoma, MEN syndromes, pituitary adenomas and thyroid nodules, with a focus on which test to order first and what to do before surgery.

Which exams are these Endocrine & Metabolic clinical cases designed for?

NEET PG, INI-CET and FMGE first of all - and they work just as well for NEET SS and USMLE-style practice. Every one of these exams now tests you with patient vignettes instead of one-line facts, so each case here is built the same way: a patient story that unfolds step by step, and you commit to a diagnosis and a plan before the next clue appears.

Are these cases based on the new exam pattern?

Yes. Look at recent NEET PG, INI-CET, FMGE and NEET SS papers - and USMLE-style questions - and you will see the shift: fewer one-liners, more real patients. The question gives you a story and asks what you would do next. That is exactly how every case on this page works: history, examination, investigations and management, revealed one step at a time.

How do I use these cases to prepare for NEET PG?

Take one case at a time and decide your answer before you reveal the next step - that is how the exam will test you. Start with the Core cases for the presentations you must know, then move to Advanced when you want the harder differentials. The 8 free cases let you try the format before you decide on Pro.

Do the cases explain the reasoning, or just give the answer?

They show the reasoning. You get the history, examination, investigations and management step by step, with the key learning point at the end - so you understand why each decision was made, not just what the answer was.
