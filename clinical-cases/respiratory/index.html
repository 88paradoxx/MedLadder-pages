What these respiratory cases test

Respiratory cases reward reading the blood gas and the bedside signs together. You decide when a COPD exacerbation with a pH of 7.28 needs non-invasive ventilation, recognise the silent chest of life-threatening asthma, grade pneumonia severity to choose between home and the ICU, and work up a suspected pulmonary embolism in the right order. Advanced cases cover home oxygen criteria, severe asthma with nasal polyps that needs biologic therapy, mediastinal masses, occupational lung disease and lung cancer screening.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many Respiratory clinical cases are there on MedLadder?

MedLadder has 38 Respiratory clinical cases: 20 Core and 18 Advanced. 8 of them are free to play, and the remaining 30 need MedLadder Pro.

Are Respiratory clinical cases free?

8 Respiratory cases are free to play without a subscription. The other 30 cases, including all Advanced cases beyond the free set, need MedLadder Pro, which also unlocks the full QBank and past papers.

Which respiratory topics matter most for NEET PG?

COPD, asthma, pneumonia, pulmonary embolism, pleural effusion, pneumothorax and tuberculosis. The cases on this page focus on management decisions such as escalating oxygen and ventilation, choosing antibiotics and picking the right first investigation.

Do the cases cover arterial blood gas interpretation?

Yes. Several cases give an ABG and ask you to act on it, for example deciding on non-invasive ventilation for hypercapnic respiratory failure in COPD. The explanation walks through the gas step by step.

How are Core and Advanced respiratory cases different?

Core cases cover the emergencies every intern must handle, such as acute asthma, COPD exacerbations and tension pneumothorax. Advanced cases test the finer points: home oxygen criteria, interstitial lung disease, sarcoidosis and lung cancer staging.

Are these respiratory medicine cases useful for MBBS exams, NEET SS and USMLE?

Yes. MBBS final-year and university exams use the same case-based format, so the Core cases double as viva and long-case practice. For NEET SS (Medicine group, including DM Pulmonary Medicine), the Advanced cases go to super-specialty depth. For USMLE Step 2 CK, every case is a next-best-step vignette in the same style. Where Indian guidelines differ from US practice, the explanation says so.

Which exams are these Respiratory clinical cases designed for?

NEET PG, INI-CET and FMGE first of all - and they work just as well for NEET SS and USMLE-style practice. Every one of these exams now tests you with patient vignettes instead of one-line facts, so each case here is built the same way: a patient story that unfolds step by step, and you commit to a diagnosis and a plan before the next clue appears.

Are these cases based on the new exam pattern?

Yes. Look at recent NEET PG, INI-CET, FMGE and NEET SS papers - and USMLE-style questions - and you will see the shift: fewer one-liners, more real patients. The question gives you a story and asks what you would do next. That is exactly how every case on this page works: history, examination, investigations and management, revealed one step at a time.

How do I use these cases to prepare for NEET PG?

Take one case at a time and decide your answer before you reveal the next step - that is how the exam will test you. Start with the Core cases for the presentations you must know, then move to Advanced when you want the harder differentials. The 8 free cases let you try the format before you decide on Pro.

Do the cases explain the reasoning, or just give the answer?

They show the reasoning. You get the history, examination, investigations and management step by step, with the key learning point at the end - so you understand why each decision was made, not just what the answer was.
