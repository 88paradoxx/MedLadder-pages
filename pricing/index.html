Questions

Is MedLadder free?

Yes, partly. The first 8 topics in every subject, 8 clinical cases in every body system, the live mock tests and 50-question previews of past papers are free. MedLadder Pro unlocks everything else.

How much does MedLadder Pro cost?

On the website, Pro costs ₹199 for 1 month, ₹499 for 3 months, ₹799 for 6 months or ₹1,299 for 1 year. In the Android app, Google Play shows the price at checkout.

Does MedLadder Pro renew automatically?

Not on the website: you pay once and Pro ends when the period is over. In the Android app, Google Play shows the renewal terms before you subscribe.

Can I get a refund?

Pro is not refundable once active, because it unlocks digital content immediately. If you were charged twice, or charged without getting Pro, email 99medladder@gmail.com within 7 days with your payment ID and it will be refunded.
