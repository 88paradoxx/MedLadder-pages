Paste the whole paper. Numbered questions (1., Q2)) or blocks separated by --- both work. Options as A. (b) c), the key as Ans: B, and Explanation: for the rest. An answer key at the end (Answer key then 1-B 2-C …) also works. Up to 300 questions per save. Nothing is saved until step 3.

Example
1. Which nerve supplies the deltoid?
A. Radial nerve
B. Axillary nerve
C. Musculocutaneous nerve
D. Suprascapular nerve
Ans: B
Explanation: The axillary nerve (C5, C6) supplies deltoid and teres minor.
