About MedLadder

An exam-preparation question bank for Indian medical graduates.

What MedLadder is

MedLadder is a question bank and practice platform for Indian medical entrance and licensing exams: NEET PG, INI-CET, FMGE and NEET SS, and for MBBS university exams. It runs on the web at medladder.top and as an Android app on Google Play.

What you can practise

  • Topic-wise MCQs across all 19 MBBS subjects, each with a written explanation. Browse them from Anatomy to Radiology.
  • Previous-year questions from recalled NEET PG, INI-CET and FMGE papers, with free 50-question previews on the PYQ pages.
  • Clinical cases organised by body system, where you choose the next step in a patient vignette and see why.
  • Live mock tests with an All-India rank and leaderboard.

Free and Pro

The first 8 topics in every subject, 8 clinical cases in every body system and the live mock tests are free. MedLadder Pro unlocks the full question bank and every clinical case.

How content is checked

Questions and explanations are reviewed by Dr Mirbilal, MBBS, MD (Pediatrics), and explanations cite the standard textbooks they draw on. Every question in the app has a Flag button so students can report a problem.

Important

MedLadder is for exam preparation and education. It is not medical advice and is not a substitute for clinical judgement or current guidelines.
