Who checks the questions on MedLadder, and how.

Medical reviewer

Dr Mirbilal, MBBS, MD (Pediatrics)
Medical Reviewer, MedLadder

Dr Mirbilal reviews MedLadder's questions and explanations across all 19 subjects and the previous-year papers: that each question has one defensible correct answer, that the explanation supports it, and that the reference points to a standard textbook.

How questions are prepared

  • Every question carries a written explanation of the correct answer.
  • Explanations cite the standard textbook they draw on, with its edition and page where available, so a student can check the claim rather than take it on trust.
  • Previous-year questions come from recalled papers, reviewed in the same way.

Reporting an error

Every question in the app has a Flag button that marks it for review. You can also write to 99medladder@gmail.com with the question and what you believe is wrong.

What MedLadder is not

MedLadder is an exam-preparation question bank for medical graduates. It is not medical advice and is not a substitute for clinical judgement or current guidelines.
