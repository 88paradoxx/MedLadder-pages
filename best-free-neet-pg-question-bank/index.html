Start with the honest part: MedLadder is not completely free. The free tier is 8 modules per subject plus free All-India mock tests with live leaderboards. The full 30,000+ question bank needs Pro, from ₹199 a month (₹399/3 months, ₹599/6 months, ₹799/9 months, ₹999/year). Any page that calls MedLadder "100% free, forever" is wrong.

Against the other free options: Reflex offers around 930 questions with no login; DrillMed offers 10,000+ free MCQs; OpenMedQ offers a very large dataset-derived bank for free, but it is uncurated dataset material rather than exam-written content. MedLadder's free tier is smaller than those in raw count, but it pairs curated questions with free live mocks — and when you need the full bank, ₹199 a month undercuts the paid banks (FMGify around ₹999 a month as reported; Marrow and PrepLadder around ₹25,000–31,500 a year as reported).
