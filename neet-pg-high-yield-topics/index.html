Methodology — read this before trusting the bands. The weightage bands below are estimated from MedLadder's own bank of 1,484 text-based NEET PG recall questions: a subject's band reflects its share of those recalls, not any official NBEMS weightage. NBEMS does not publish subject-wise weightage, and recall collections skew towards memorable questions, so treat the bands as a revision guide, not as the exam's marking scheme.

Frequently asked questions

What makes a topic high-yield here?

Its share of the 1,484 text-based NEET PG recall questions in MedLadder's bank. Subjects with more recalls rank higher.

Is this an official high-yield list?

No. It is a recall-based ranking from one bank's collection, not an NBEMS list. Use it to order revision, not to skip subjects.

Do image-based questions count in this ranking?

No. Image-based recalls are excluded because the bank's images are currently unavailable, so image-heavy subjects may rank lower here than in the real exam.
