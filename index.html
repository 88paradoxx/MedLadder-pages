How MedLadder works

Pick a subject, then a topic, and answer one question at a time. In practice mode you see straight away whether you were right, why the correct option is correct, and the textbook the explanation comes from. A timed test runs the same questions against the clock, the way the real exam does, and shows your score and every missed question at the end.

Your answers are saved to your account, so the My progress page can show how many questions you have answered, your accuracy, your strongest and weakest subjects, and the next topic to practise. Questions you want to revisit can be bookmarked and practised together later.

Free and Pro access

One topic in every subject can be tried without an account. A free account opens the first eight topics of each subject and keeps your progress. MedLadder Pro opens every topic and the previous-year papers, with plans from one month to one year, paid securely through Razorpay by UPI, card or net banking.

Who it is for

MedLadder is built for MBBS graduates preparing for NEET PG and INI-CET, for foreign medical graduates taking FMGE, and for postgraduates preparing for NEET SS. It works in any browser on a phone or laptop, and as an Android app on Google Play.
