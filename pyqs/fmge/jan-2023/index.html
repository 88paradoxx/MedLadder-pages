Question 19

The first heart sound (S1), the "lub" of the "lub-dub," is produced primarily by the closure of the atrioventricular (mitral and tricuspid) valves. Question 25

A 65-year-old man undergoing evaluation for suspected renal dysfunction has his creatinine clearance measured. Which statement best describes the significance and interpretation of creatinine clearance?

Show answer and explanation

Correct answer: A

Creatinine clearance is a widely used clinical measure to estimate the Glomerular Filtration Rate (GFR). Consequently, it remains the standard-of-care proxy for GFR in everyday medical practice.

Question 29

Hemoglobin with iron in ferric form is :

Normally, the iron present in hemoglobin is in the ferrous (Fe²⁺) state, which allows reversible binding of oxygen. Ferric iron cannot bind oxygen, and its presence also increases the oxygen affinity of the remaining ferrous heme groups, thereby impairing oxygen release to tissues.
