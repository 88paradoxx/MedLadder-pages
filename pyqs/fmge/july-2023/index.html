Show answer and explanation

Correct answer: B

Cardiolipin is a unique phospholipid that is found almost exclusively in the inner mitochondrial membrane. It accounts for approximately 20% of the total lipid composition in this membrane. Cardiolipin is essential for the structural integrity and optimal function of various mitochondrial proteins, including those involved in the electron transport chain (such as Complex IV) and the ATP synthase complex.
