How to use previous year questions

Start with the most recent two or three papers of your exam under timed conditions, then mark every question you got wrong or guessed. Read the explanation, find the topic in the MedLadder question bank, and practise that topic until you score well on it. Go back to older papers in the last month before the exam: by then you know the syllabus, and older papers show which concepts are asked again and again in new forms.

MBBS, NEET PG, FMGE and INI-CET draw heavily on the same core subjects, so a question recalled from one exam is often useful practice for the others.

Frequently asked questions

What are previous year questions (PYQs)?

Previous year questions are questions recalled from past medical entrance exams. Practising them shows the topics and question styles each exam repeats.

Are these official exam papers?

No. NBEMS does not release official papers, so these pages use memory-based recall questions, each with an answer and, where available, an explanation.

Which exams are covered?

NEET PG (2018 to 2025), FMGE (December 2020 to July 2025) and INI-CET (November 2020 to May 2025), with 4994 text-based recall questions in total.
