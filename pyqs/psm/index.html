Sydenham's chorea is a major neurological manifestation of acute rheumatic fever, resulting from an autoimmune reaction involving the basal ganglia. It presents with choreiform (rapid, involuntary, purposeless) movements, emotional lability, and hypotonia. Question 6

Echinococcus granulosus is most commonly found in which of the following animals?

Show answer and explanation

Correct answer: A

Echinococcus granulosus is a cestode (tapeworm) that causes cystic echinococcosis (hydatid disease) in humans. The parasite has a complex two-host lifecycle. The definitive host, where the adult tapeworm lives in the intestine, is a canid, most commonly the dog. The adult worm in the dog's intestine produces eggs, which are passed in the feces. The intermediate hosts are typically herbivores, such as sheep, goats, cattle, and pigs, which become infected by ingesting the eggs. In the intermediate host, the eggs hatch, and the larvae migrate to organs like the liver and lungs, where they form the characteristic hydatid cysts. The lifecycle is completed when a dog ingests the cyst-containing organs of an infected intermediate host. Humans are accidental intermediate hosts, becoming infected by ingesting the eggs from soil or food contaminated with dog feces, or through direct contact with infected dogs. Cats are definitive hosts for other tapeworms like Taenia taeniaeformis.

Show answer and explanation

Correct answer: C

The Nalgonda technique is a simple, cost-effective water treatment method developed in India specifically for the de fluoridation of drinking water at the community or household level. The process involves the sequential addition of two common chemicals to the fluoride-contaminated water. The first is lime (calcium hydroxide), which is added to provide the necessary alkalinity for the reaction. The second and key chemical is alum (aluminum sulfate). For a variable to be a confounder, it must meet three specific criteria :1. It must be a risk factor for the disease (outcome) among the unexposed.2. Consanguineous marriage, or marriage between close biological relatives, increases the risk of having offspring with autosomal recessive genetic disorders.
