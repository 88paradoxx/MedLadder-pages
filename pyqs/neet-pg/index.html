Frequently asked questions

Are these the official NEET PG papers?

No. NBEMS does not release official papers, so these are memory-based recall questions collected from each exam, each with an answer and, where available, an explanation.

How many NEET PG previous year questions are here?

There are 1484 text-based recall questions across the 2018 to 2025 papers. Image-based questions are excluded for now because the bank's images are currently unavailable.

How do I use these papers?

Open a year paper, answer each question, and check the explanation. You can also download each paper as a PDF, or take a free All-India mock test from the mock lobby.
