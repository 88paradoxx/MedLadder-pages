The weightage bands below are estimated from MedLadder's own bank of 1,484 text-based NEET PG recall questions: a subject's band reflects its share of those recalls, not any official NBEMS weightage. NBEMS does not publish subject-wise weightage, and recall collections skew towards memorable questions, so treat the bands as a revision guide, not as the exam's marking scheme.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the official NEET PG syllabus or weightage?

No. The subject list follows the standard NEET PG syllabus, but the weightage bands are MedLadder's own estimate from its 1,484 NEET PG recall questions. NBEMS does not publish subject-wise weightage.

How were the bands calculated?

Each subject's share of the 1,484 text-based NEET PG recalls in the bank: 10% or more is Very high, 5–10% High, 3–5% Moderate, below 3% Lower. Image-based recalls are excluded because the bank's images are currently unavailable.

Where do I practise each subject?

Every subject row links to its subject question bank and to its NEET PG previous-year questions page.
